# Direct seven-cat source without terminal resource

The complete abstract seven-cat source is raw-valid without the A/B/C terminal instrument, but its exact full-noise conditional loss is **8.933650189341478e-4** at acceptance **0.8202806420127153**. It has **c1=0.3166666666666667**, with 14 harmful accepted single-fault components. This is about 30 times worse than the 2.9720782263202955e-5 abstract parent that includes the verified terminal square. Removing that terminal therefore does not produce a competitive direct-output control in this construction.

`source_direct.qasm` is a complete physical-noise circuit on **abstract connectivity**: 83 CX and 18 clocks, ordered outputs `[0,1,2,3,4,5,6]`. The raw ideal check reports all six Steane stabilizers +1, ideal infidelity zero. The design retains the initial magic encoder, both Steane precheck blocks, both seven-member cat logical checks, all shared cat flags implemented through those precheck blocks, and the extra physical q35 cat-parity flag from `SOTA_DETECT_20261028/deep_extra_best/flag_9_0_5`. The nine source detector rows include six precheck rows, two seven-cat parity rows and the extra q35 flag. All seven final T-dagger gates occupy the last source clock17, so no extra output wait is introduced after the source checks.

`source_build.py` independently regenerates the pre-terminal source operations from the earlier `build_joint_flags` construction using the same `(0,1),(2,5),(4,6)` flag pairs. `extract.py` matches their gate/record identities to the optimized parent schedule and retains the actual early q35 flag; it removes the late A/B/C resource and terminal instrument. It verifies the required gate multiset and exact source-detector rows, checks clock collisions, and calculates raw correctness, all physical single-fault components and full-noise expectation. Source-only extraction is not the unrecovered historical DEEP artifact and is not a native grid candidate.

The largest accepted single-fault location contributions to c1 are CX29→1 at clock12 (**0.1**), CX29→14 at clock11 (**0.0666667**), and CX17→3 at clock7 (**0.05**); the other five gate-location groups are in `source_direct.result.json`. Their first-order contribution alone is approximately 3.17e-4 at p=.001, before finite-p conditioning and higher orders. Those diagnostics are not an additive decomposition of the exact conditional loss. The complete source-specific detector and fault records are preserved in the receipt.
