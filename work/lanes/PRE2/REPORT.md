# PRE2 — pre-stage redesign and L5 merge

The best complete circuit is `compact_narrow_m`: it keeps the v14 cultivation prefix and replaces the L5 B+C preparation with the BC2 13-layer, 73-CX fault-tolerant witness. It passes every judge gate at 1e6 shots and has a three-seed 1e8-shot mean of **8.85595e-5**, below the v14 mean **1.03732e-4**. No submission was made.

All commands used `.venv/bin/python` with `OMP_NUM_THREADS=1`, `OPENBLAS_NUM_THREADS=1`, and `MKL_NUM_THREADS=1`. The full-circuit merge is generated by `build_candidates.py`; its prefix schedule is the judge schedule, and the two first-use helper resets are the measured-init rewrite used by v14.
