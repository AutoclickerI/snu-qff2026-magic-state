# Published routes toward the retained-Steane output floor

**Recommendation:** prioritize a jointly synthesized, verified Steane output round for the existing cultivation prefix; investigate the older **two-block Daguerre–Kim switch** as the complete alternative; keep **morphed-code preparation** as a smaller-footprint exploratory alternative. The headline cubic errors of Gao–Yuan–Yao and cultivation are **not predictions for this judge**. They condition on terminal syndrome information that is unavailable from an unmeasured output. No paper reviewed here establishes a circuit beating the supplied leader under the complete contest noise model and grid constraints.

This report uses the PDFs and text in [papers/](papers/), with PDF page numbers (not journal pagination). [MANIFEST.json](papers/MANIFEST.json) records downloaded versions and hashes. `NR` means not reported as a scalar/count in the cited source; `unverified` means not checked in a complete contest compilation. An inferred finite-p ratio is explicitly distinguished from an asymptotic coefficient. The supplied comparison—our approximately `1.10e-4`, leader `6.29e-5`, leader `169 CX`, acceptance `0.546`—comes from the task, not a refreshed leaderboard. Current repository findings also contain newer local candidates; these are used as evidence, not official standings.

**Metric and boundary accounting.** The judge returns an unmeasured Steane block, performs ideal lookup correction, and scores `L = p(Z_L) + [p(X_L)+p(Y_L)]/2`; it does not reject residual output syndrome. See [judge reference](../../../docs/reference/judge.md) and [exact checker](../../../upstream/snu-qff2026-problem/tprep/exact.py). A paper's `Pr(logical Pauli failure)` can differ from this infidelity, and `Pr(failure | ideal final syndrome = 0)` differs more substantially. The `H`- and `T`-type states in these papers are Clifford equivalent: Chamberland–Cross Eq. (7), PDF p.3, gives the conversion `H S†`, up to phase. That conversion is legal but its physical gates and waiting must be charged.

For a specified **physical** partition into pre-output locations and output-tail locations, write

`L_STANDARD = L_pre-only + L_tail-only + Δ_cross`.

Here each term uses the actual circuit's acceptance/decoder; `Δ_cross` is defined as the difference, including changed acceptance. Correctable single faults before and after the boundary can combine into logical errors, so **cross terms can be order p²**. They cannot be dropped on the grounds that each isolated region has quadratic logical loss. A source's corrected error may already include its last gates; adding their full tail again double counts them. For terminal-projector papers, removing the projector changes the accepted ensemble; it is not just an extra final depolarizing channel.

For identical masses `(a,b,c)`, the pair calculation is `21 [ (a+b)²/2 + c² + 2bc ]`, using the judge's logical costs; the table follows by substitution. These are **architecture-conditioned tail models**. The repository's [AUDIT report](../AUDIT/REPORT.md) has not proved a positive circuit-independent lower bound from the judge code alone. The `1.116e-5` figure therefore should not be advertised as such a theorem. The practical direct-versus-Bell saving is still substantial: `(58.5666667−25.6666667)10^-6 = 3.29e-5` before other circuit changes.

For table entries with no executable STANDARD circuit, `U + D + Δ` means an **inferred direct-output adaptation**: `U` is its as-yet-unmeasured pre-output loss under STANDARD, and `Δ` includes cross terms, routing, changed checks/decoder and conversion. This notation exposes an unknown; it is not a point estimate. Explicit numerical “budgets” below are design targets under stated assumptions, not predicted experimental performance.

**Judge limits checked in source.** [qasm.py, constants](../../../upstream/snu-qff2026-problem/tprep/qasm.py:28) enforces `200,000 bytes`, `5,000` semicolon statements, and `measure+reset ≤1,000`; [problem.md](../../../upstream/snu-qff2026-problem/problem.md), §§1.1–1.6 and 3, requires the `6×6` nearest-neighbour grid, CX-only entanglers, supported gates/class, fixed output and acceptance `≥0.5`. No separate raw T-count cap was found. [exact.py:35](../../../upstream/snu-qff2026-problem/tprep/exact.py:35) sets **`MAX_RANK=22` for the magic/postselection X-mask span**, not 22 T gates; [check.py:157](../../../upstream/snu-qff2026-problem/tprep/check.py:157) separately invokes the xtim simulable-class check. Commuting pushed π/8 axes, exact ideal output and permitted classical processing must all survive compilation. Measurement-conditioned arbitrary gates and nonlinear accept/decoder logic cannot simply be pasted into QASM; the permitted XOR detector and logical-frame annotations are much narrower. A physical gauge frame is not automatically a permitted logical frame.

In the tables, **size U** means the complete lowered, routed statement/MR counts remain unverified; no paper's abstract gate count certifies those limits. Small fixed circuits plausibly fit the textual caps, but placement, class and acceptance are the consequential checks. Native CZ can be lowered to CX with Hadamards, and CCZ can be synthesized exactly; their added noise must be included, so neither is automatically disqualified solely by its notation.

**Local evidence that changes the ranking.** These measurements were already present in other lanes; I read their receipts and recalculated arithmetic rather than rerunning their Monte Carlo workloads. They are not results of new LIT circuit experiments.

- **RM reproduction and retained-output repair:** [RM_PROTO report](../RM_PROTO/REPORT.md), [judge checks](../RM_PROTO/judge_limits.log), [three-seed MC](../RM_PROTO/va_ta0_PA_mc_score.json) reproduce the published destructive-ED behaviour and expose its mismatch. The random gauge signs in Gao Eq.A9 cannot be dropped: postselecting all signs would keep only `1/8` even without noise; coherent correction can instead use `7 CX`, as verified locally. The corrected canonical `89-CX` circuit has loss `.0126091`, acceptance `.503075`, with up to `21` terminal idle steps, RM_PROTO report §3/judge log. The reduced-source plus verified-round candidate has seed-0 loss **`8.48288854e-5`**, three-seed mean **`8.51894583e-5`**, mean acceptance **`.64293977`**, at `10^8` shots per seed (MC receipt). These are grid-free, not official or routed scores.
- **The tail addition error is measurable:** [RM regions](../RM_PROTO/va_ta0_PA_regions.json) gives second-order tail `2.56666667e-5`, rest `4.34586832e-5`; [RM report](../RM_PROTO/REPORT.md) gives total `7.25775599e-5`. Thus inferred mixed contribution is `7.25775599e-5−2.56666667e-5−4.34586832e-5 =3.4522100e-6`, already order p². All-order noise-isolation values are tail `2.58803871e-5`, rest `5.23988114e-5`; subtracting from the three-seed full mean gives `6.9102598e-6` residual (different MC draws, so not an exact coefficient). This is why “paper error + floor” is not sufficient even after the correct boundary is chosen.
- **Compact mixed/ZX extraction is not a ready win:** [SEARCH evaluation receipt](../SEARCH/evaluations/summary.json), ideal encoded input with noisy extraction only, gives PECOS `28 CX` mixed checks: reused `10q/16 steps`, loss `3.41114e-4`; separate `13q/14 steps`, `3.00995e-4`. Poór ZX: separate `15q/28CX/11 steps`, `3.23500e-4`; reused `11q/19 steps`, `6.07072e-4`. These screens have no routing and no source-preparation faults. They reject those schedules, not the general synthesis methods.
- **The best direct round still has a geometry problem:** [FINDINGS](../../../docs/FINDINGS.md), rows “Steane-type final round”, “Block D”, “Smaller FT joint B+C prep”, and “v14 loss by region”, record a grid-free `28q/48CX` quadratic round at `3.2324e-5`, and an extra-verification `35q` round around `2.90e-5`. But grid scheduling serializes preparations and creates source×round errors. A tested smaller joint-prep full circuit had three-seed mean `1.153e-4`, acceptance `.544`; a subsequent joint-prep variant's mean is `1.0497e-4`, with further local changes around `1.03817e-4` over redraws. These are concrete reasons to optimize correlated fault sets and start times rather than assume the ideal round can be inserted unchanged.
- **Cultivation is already in use:** [circuit reference](../../../docs/reference/circuit.md) describes the present injection/double-cat prefix. [FINDINGS](../../../docs/FINDINGS.md), “v12 loss by construction stage”, assigns `1.46e-5` to the pre-stage and `9.76e-5` to the final cycle with each pair split half to each location. This is an attribution, **not an independently composable pre-output error**. Extra prefix checks have already worsened full scores. Wan is a more exact analysis of this family, not a separate route to rank beside GSJ.

The strongest practical conclusion is narrower than “find a higher-order preparation”: the literature already suppresses the *terminal-postselected* component well below our output floor, while our scored errors mostly arise from a physical final round and its interaction with earlier faults. A new circuit must make that round cheap under the actual grid and idle model. Published exponents alone cannot establish it.
