## 1. Reopen BFLAG only with decoder-safe residues and the correct objective

### The exact restriction

`work/lanes/BFLAG/synth_bc.py`, lines 141–164, represents a resource X fault by four B bits — three syndrome bits and weight parity — followed by the literal C-difference and flag bits. Its F1 clauses at lines 151–153 require:

\[
\text{all detectors zero}\implies s=0.
\]

This is stronger than requiring that the delivered X error be corrected by the final decoder. The `--soft` option does **not** soften F1: its `esc` variable is added only to the subsequent robust clauses. `lns3.py` explicitly calls its model “F1 hard” and calls `S.build(..., robust=False)` at line 42; setting `robust=False` leaves F1 intact. Thus the 14 fresh BFLAG synthesis/LNS pipelines did not search all decoder-correctable residue classes.

The distinction is already expressible using the repository's own `proxy.dangerous(s)` at `proxy.py:25–26`. With the existing Steane column convention, the safe signatures are

\[
\{0,9,10,11,12,13,14,15\}.
\]

Each signature contains eight physical X patterns. Current F1 permits eight patterns; decoder correctability permits 64. This is a statement about the admissible single-fault class, **not** an eightfold performance gain.

### Independent certificate and minimal implementation

`correctable_residue_probe.py` enumerates all 128 X patterns, applies the syndrome-selected single-X correction, and checks whether the result is in the X-stabilizer span generated by masks 85, 102 and 120. It proves agreement among the actual correction criterion, `not proxy.dangerous(s)`, and

\[
s_3 = s_0\lor s_1\lor s_2.
\]

`correctable_residue_certificate.json` records all 128 cases. The replacement for the four F1 clauses is just:

```python
m.AddBoolOr(dif + [lit.Not(), s[3].Not(), s[0], s[1], s[2]])
for k in range(3):
    m.AddBoolOr(dif + [lit.Not(), s[k].Not(), s[3]])
```

These clauses preserve first-order decoder safety for this X-residue endpoint contract. They do not waive full-circuit first-order verification, state preparation, cell-release, detector or judge constraints.

### The important historical qualification

This is **not an untried physical principle**. `FRESH/REPORT_prior_architecture.md:9–27` already describes correlated rank-three resources with accepted correctable B residues. Its physical R45 builds scored **8.86e-5–1.05e-4**, despite negligible first-order logical loss and only 170–185 CX. The recorded failure mechanism was precisely a surviving weight-one B residue combining with another late X fault.

What is newly justified is a **bounded search around the current r3/v36g resource with a different objective**, rather than claiming that the older relaxed-residue designs were overlooked or successful. The current hard F1 search does not prove this expanded local space is exhausted; the historical failures sharply lower the prior probability of a large gain.

### The objective must change at the same time

`lns3.py:25–35` scores

\[
J=X2+K_ZR_Z+K_A\,\mathrm{acc1}.
\]

`proxy.py:182–196` separately computes **`X2leak`** for dangerous pairs whose individual detector signature is already zero. The current LNS objective excludes it. This omission is harmless only to the extent that the accepted residue channel is effectively fixed across its restricted candidates. Once F1 changes, the omitted quantity is one of the main costs being traded. Adding `X2leak` is the minimum diagnostic repair; it still misses the full cultivation-prefix and suffix cross terms and should not be used as the final ranking criterion.

The practical experiment is to release a 4–6-wire, 3–5-layer neighborhood, preserve the current v36g exterior, allow decoder-safe classes, and rank a small Pareto set using the whole-circuit all-orders evaluator. Keep the current clean candidate as a feasible incumbent. Reject any candidate with nonzero whole-circuit first-order logical loss, invalid ideal detectors, insufficient acceptance, or a physical loss increase. A fixed step budget is appropriate because the earlier relaxed-residue architecture already failed badly.

### New bounded current-circuit screen

`current_resource_deletion_probe.py` tested **all 1,596 one/two-CX deletions** among r3's 56 preparation CXs. It retained the exact rank-three pure endpoint and screened resource Pauli singles using the existing proxy on the current v36g surroundings, including q6's earlier data guard.

Results:

- 23 variants preserve the pure endpoint.
- 22 pass the proxy's zero first-order logical-loss screen.
- None creates extra accepted residue compared with baseline, so this screen does **not** give a positive witness for the F1 relaxation.
- The lowest local `X2 + X2leak` variant removes q4→q5 at ticks 41 and 42. It improves that diagnostic by only **7.1111e-8**, while worsening `RZ` by **0.00106667**. No whole-circuit gain is claimed; the exterior can easily reverse that diagnostic ordering.

The complete census is `resource_deletion_certificate.json`; eight diagnostic QASM/JSON variants are included as `resource_delete_00` through `_07`. They are **not finalists** and were not all-orders priced or officially promoted.

## 2. Expand one synthesis boundary and its gate alphabet, with a strict first gate

### What the published closures actually cover

The AENC all-orders optimum is explicitly over **CX-only** encoders on seven fixed A cells, within fixed release/coupling windows, with up to 16 CX (`AENC/REPORT.md:35–39`). BFLAG tracks three classical X rows and chooses directed nearest-neighbor CXs; it keeps B→C at tick 44 outside the synthesized preparation (`synth_bc.py:1–6`, `67–82`). These are substantial searched spaces, but they do not imply optimality over arbitrary Clifford preparations or a region spanning the preparation/verification boundary.

The concrete extension is a 4–6-wire Clifford-window compiler which can use H/S and CX, and which can cross one formerly fixed interface. Start with the B/C preparation's last few layers plus the B→C layer at tick 44. The desired final ideal state is still exactly

\[
|0_L\rangle_B\otimes|0\rangle_C^{\otimes7},
\]

with the same required classical records and the same B→D/readout exterior. The intermediate correlated state \(\sum_g|g\rangle_B|g\rangle_C\) at tick 43 need not be reproduced if the combined region has the right endpoint and fault behavior. This removes a concrete internal state boundary without assuming a wholly new accepted instrument.

For a first CX-only implementation, extend the optimized depth through tick 44, set the endpoint generator columns to `col(i)` on B and zero on C, and make the endpoint X-fault signatures the B syndrome/parity plus **literal C X bits**, rather than the pre-B→C difference. The native cell occupancy of A couplings and helper resets at tick 44 remains binding. Later, replace the X-row representation by a full symplectic tableau to allow non-CSS intermediates.

### Why this is not the closed H-frame waiting proposal

The repository correctly rejected a pure H-frame change during a depolarizing wait (`IDEAS/REPORT.md`, row 11): isotropic depolarization is covariant. A global frame-conjugate circuit with the same physical noisy instrument is not a new candidate.

The proposed search must change the entangler realization, intermediate stabilizer geometry, preparation/verification ordering, or fault-to-detector map. A one-qubit Clifford occupying an **already charged live idle** has the same one-qubit depolarizing location as that idle under the official model; it may enlarge the available synthesis language without adding that location. This does not make arbitrary extra Cliffords free. A gate in a previously inactive post-measurement period creates a new noisy operation; any added tick can expose other live data; RX/MX folding only applies where the official parser allows it.

### Falsifiable first gate and stop condition

Pick a single 4–6-wire window and enumerate or solve for exact ideal endpoint/instrument equivalence, including all boundary Pauli inputs and the classical record map. Reject transformations equivalent only by an isotropic-noise frame conjugation. Compare the changed fault-to-record/data responses and the actual native schedule. Only a physically distinct candidate that passes the ideal/whole-circuit first-order checks should reach a full-noise contraction.

No new circuit has been produced for this compiler expansion in this audit. It is a **gated implementation direction**, not an improvement claim, an established new algorithm, or evidence that a simple Clifford insertion wins. It should be one compiler work item, not several differently named versions counted toward the requested ten candidates. Parent review separately addresses the stale AENC exterior table and A-encoder/A-decoder/data-guard joint boundary; this proposal should not duplicate that item without a distinct physical witness.

## 3. Four-T measured-classical RCCX: a real primitive, a negative first application

### Scope of the exact identity

A coherent, phase-exact Toffoli is stronger than necessary for two already Z-measured physical control records followed by measurement of the AND target. The following relative-phase Toffoli uses **4 T-type gates and 3 CX**:

```text
H target
T target
CX b,target
TDG target
CX a,target
T target
CX b,target
TDG target
H target
```

`rccx_probe.py` checks all eight computational-basis inputs. It gives precisely the Toffoli output bits; its input-dependent phases are not all equal. For Z-dephased physical controls and a measured target, those phases disappear from the classical instrument. The same identity is **not** valid as a coherent Toffoli or an arbitrary uncompute substitution.

The former seven-T Toffoli suggestion would take a 15-T circuit to 22 T. This implementation takes it to **19 T**, below the current sparse evaluator's **20-rotation dense sign-kernel cap** (`MAIN/sparse/sparse_walsh.py:103`). That cap is an evaluator implementation limit, not a contest rule. Being below it does not by itself prove every other sparse-kernel condition, runtime or memory requirement is satisfied. No full 19-T all-orders contraction was needed after the cheaper policy oracle failed.

### Complete native placement on v36g

The emitted circuit uses q12 (Z record c310 measured at tick 11) and q19 (Z record c3 measured at tick 10) as controls; both physical records remain available. The target q18 was measured at tick 8 and is reset again at tick 19.

| Tick | Added native operation |
|---:|---|
| 9 | RX q18 |
| 10 | T q18 |

All CXs are nearest-neighbor and there is no operation collision. The native circuit remains 47 ticks. The target-end H gates are folded using the actual RX/MX rules; there is no claim that its interior gates are free.

The candidate removes detector rows 3 and 75, which require c3=0 and c310=0, and adds c316=0. Its ideal acceptance predicate is **NOT(c3 AND c310)**, accepting 00, 01 and 10 and rejecting 11. This is a physical realization of nonlinear classical postselection without conditional instructions.

### Exact cost-free policy oracle: direct loss does not improve

Parent review independently priced this exact Boolean predicate without the added physical gates. Using zero-reference relaxations,

\[
P(d_3=0\;\lor\;d_{75}=0,\mathrm{others}=0)
=P(\mathrm{drop}\ d_3)+P(\mathrm{drop}\ d_{75})-P(\mathrm{all}\ 0).
\]

The same identity is applied to the unnormalized loss numerator before dividing by acceptance. Receipt: `../policy/nand_oracle.json`.

| Quantity | v36g | Cost-free NAND oracle | Change |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Acceptance | 0.5015149656065663 | 0.5028576971064658 | +0.0013427314998995 |
| Conditional loss | 3.836054467100275e-5 | 3.836362258928492e-5 | **+3.07791828217e-9** |

The first application therefore has **no direct loss gain even before implementation cost**. The physical sample also fails acceptance. The free policy can be described as an acceptance trade, but its physical realization has not been shown to provide useful acceptance budget. Keep the primitive, its truth certificate, and its legal placement as reusable implementation evidence; do not advertise this candidate as a physical optimization success or run expensive confirmation merely because the primitive is elegant.

For any future record pair, the first gate should be a joint accepted-sector oracle; only a sufficiently favorable policy or budget trade warrants building and pricing its physical implementation. Control records must remain available and Z-dephased, the target must be measured/discarded, and all added noise, measurements, idles and T-simulation cost must be counted.

## Closed routes and limitations to carry into the full report

The old rank-four 14-T idea is now an implemented negative result: T14's complete grid-legal t14a scores **1.7302051e-4**, about 4.2 times the then-baseline v36c. Its source identifies even-parity-holder faults and suffix cross terms; a nongrid diagnostic X check does not restore a competitive full circuit. Do not present the original 14-T transformation as a fresh path toward 1e-5. The measured single construction and diagnostic ablations do not constitute a universal bound on every future architecture.

The correctable-residue principle was previously tried and lost; the new concrete observation is that current BFLAG's hard F1 and old proxy jointly exclude a carefully retargeted finite-noise search. The Clifford compiler direction has no witness yet. The four-T RCCX identity and native placement are verified, while its first policy is a negative result. These distinctions are necessary if the report is to meet the user's requirement for rigorous candidates rather than ten plausible-sounding promises.
