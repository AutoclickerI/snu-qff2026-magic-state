MAIN NEW FINDING: THE CLAIMED p/30 QUANTUM IS FALSE
-------------------------------------------------
docs/PLAN.md line63 rejects every c1!=0 direction because one harmful
two-qubit Pauli allegedly costs at least p/15 times1/2 =p/30.
That statement is not a consequence of the rules and is false for legal
circuits in this very circuit family.

The correct first-order contribution of one location/Pauli is

    (physical fault probability) * (accepted branch mass)
                                * (decoded loss on that accepted branch).

Both factors after the physical fault probability matter. A non-Clifford
fault need not produce just one logical Pauli on the target. Its accepted
branch can have probability below1 and its decoded loss can be below1/2.

Two actual circuit witnesses are supplied, with exact arithmetic in
Q[w]/(w^4+1), w=exp(i*pi/4), and actual official admissibility checks:

1. v31s_a_decode_no_detectors.qasm
   Same quantum gates as the validated v31s+A decoder; remove all DETECTOR
   declarations. Noiseless output remains exactly the target. Acceptance1.
   Fault: XI after CX13->7, native instruction23.
   Conditional decoded loss EXACTLY1/4; accepted mass EXACTLY1.
   Single-location contribution: p/60 =1.666666666666667e-5 at p=.001.
   Actual official1e4-shot check: all four gatesPASS; acceptance1;
   score.09665. This is deliberately a bad circuit, not an optimization.
   Unmodified official exact.run + the actual decoder observable independently
   gives single-fault decoded loss.25000000000000017.

2. v31s_a_decode_drop2_15.qasm
   Remove only detector rows2 and15 (zero-based), corresponding to
   {c2} and {c15,c17,c19,c20,c24}. Keep all quantum operations and other
   detector declarations unchanged.
   Fault: XI after CX7->6, native instruction108.
   Accepted mass EXACTLY1/4; accepted conditional decoded loss EXACTLY1/2;
   unnormalized accepted loss EXACTLY1/8.
   Single-location contribution: p/120 =8.333333333333333e-6 at p=.001.
   Actual official1e4-shot check: all four gatesPASS; acceptance.5509;
   score.0002722817208203648 (SEM.00015715911577165914).
   Total first-order contribution is.0003, so this is also uncompetitive.

Neither witness is proposed for submission. They refute the general reason
used to close a search direction. They do not establish that a competitive
c1!=0 circuit has already been found, nor that every existing c1-based
rejection was mistaken.

METHOD1 — FINITE-p FAULT BUDGET INSTEAD OF A BOOLEAN c1 FILTER
-----------------------------------------------------------
This is the new, justified search-space reopening from this lane.

Use c1=0 as a fast preference, not a definition of feasibility. For each
proposal, evaluate accepted loss weights of each real fault. If a proposal
removes enough noisy gates, long waits, or large resource blocks, it is
permitted to spend a small weighted first-order budget. The total
conditional loss at p=.001 decides whether it is an improvement.

A safe early rejection can be much stronger than an unsupported per-fault
quantum. Let channel j have total nonidentity probability p_j, alternative
fault probability q_jf, conditional acceptance a_jf and accepted decoded
loss l_jf. Let A0 be ideal acceptance and

    P0 = product_j(1-p_j)
    P1 = sum_jf P0*q_jf/(1-p_j)
    N1 = sum_jf P0*q_jf/(1-p_j) * a_jf*l_jf
    A1 = sum_jf P0*q_jf/(1-p_j) * a_jf.

All remaining error-pattern loss contributions are nonnegative. A valid
upper bound on total acceptance is

    Aupper = P0*A0 + A1 + (1-P0-P1),

and therefore full L >= N1/Aupper. Include a nonzero ideal loss term if a
diagnostic uses an incorrect ideal circuit; admissible candidates have0.
This formula is exact for independent modeled channels. Aggregated idle
channels must use their exact finite-p probabilities, not n*p.

finite_p_guard.py implements this screen with floating arithmetic, so its
reported numbers are numerical screens, not interval-arithmetic proofs:
  good v31s+A: lower bound numerical0;
  two-detector witness: lower bound2.1108605188914424e-4;
  no-detector witness: lower bound.05189469649921476.
Thus it immediately rejects both deliberately bad witnesses while keeping
the actual freedom to explore small accepted first-order risk.

Strict promotion condition:
  exact ideal correctness; official format/class; A(p)>=.5; finite-p
  full-circuit L lower than the current best; official runtime check.
Strict early rejection: a certified N1/Aupper already exceeds the incumbent.
No promised performance improvement is attached to merely allowing c1!=0.


METHOD2 — JOINT AFFINE FLAG/FRAME OPTIMIZATION, WITH AN ORACLE FIRST
----------------------------------------------------------------
This is a design methodology worth testing where the newest acceptance
budget binds, not a fresh claim that C-only decoding was missed.

The legal classical family is H*m=b for acceptance and two affine logical
Pauli-frame maps F*m+c. It is larger than selecting a subset of existing
DETECTOR rows. For two existing deterministic flags d1,d2, keeping only
d1 XOR d2 accepts sectors00 and11; neither 'keep both' nor dropping just
one check represents that policy. The quantum circuit and its gate noise
can remain unchanged. The choice must be priced on joint sector masses
and corrected X/Y moments, including actual noise correlations.

Do NOT claim that arbitrary nonlinear Bayesian acceptance or nonlinear
logical correction is free. Official declarations implement affine parity
constraints and affine logical Pauli frames only. A FRAME modification by
a detector already forced to0 has exactly no effect on accepted runs;
frame and acceptance choices must be changed jointly to create a new policy.

Suggested experiment: expose at most6-8 joint final-A/early-check/wait-flag
bits on the newest architecture. Retain all other constraints. Compute
P(m), unnormalized corrected X and Y moments at all orders. First solve an
optimistic arbitrary-sector/best-IXYZ oracle under A>=.5; if that oracle
does not beat the incumbent, close this fixed-circuit classical direction.
If it can, enumerate/legal-synthesize affine subspaces and frame maps.

Important negative evidence: review5_search already exposed all7 C bits on
v30f and even an arbitrary Boolean acceptance plus free best-Pauli oracle
gave no improvement. Every nonzero sector was worse; the best had risk
.002258368, versus all-zero5.893774e-5. That is a closure for the fixed
C-only circuit, not for joint A/B/wait records or a newly synthesized
resource. The root lane owns the current v32p echo/subspace experiment;
this lane did not duplicate it or claim its results.

Strict promotion condition: an actual legal affine policy improves full
L at A>=.5 and passes exact ideal/noiseless correctness and runtime.
Do not promote the unconstrained oracle itself.


METHOD3 — MAKE THE EVALUATOR FOLLOW THE LEGAL BRANCH CONTRACT
-----------------------------------------------------------
This is a search-enabling implementation change, not a physical gain by
itself. The main sparse entry point and AGENTS currently restrict their
scope to all-used-qubit barriers, no FRAME/POSTSELECT, and ideal A0=1.
Those are evaluator assumptions, not contest rules.

The official problem1.3/1.6/2.1 permits declared affine logical frames,
detectors deterministic conditional on POSTSELECT, nonzero reference
parities, and ideal acceptance below1 provided noisy acceptance is>=.5.
The local review6_tail frontend already supports affine frames, but its
sparse prepare() still explicitly rejects POSTSELECT and assumes A0=1
and zero reference signs. Its parser interprets any barrier as a global
tick; partial barrier schedules therefore need actual native scheduling.

An evaluator extension should use the official native instruction schedule,
track actual accepted branch normalization A0, include reference signs and
explicit affine selection projectors, and preserve the declared noisy
frame rather than replacing it with an ideally equivalent one. Validate
every extension against the actual judge on a tiny legal witness before
using it for circuit ranking. This avoids silently banning legal instrument
targets because the current scoring prototype cannot represent them.

This is distinct from claiming that every such new policy will improve.
Uniform random stabilizer branch postselection often costs1/2 and leaves
no noisy acceptance margin. Bias from non-Clifford states can change that
probability; use actual A(p), not a generic factor-of-two assumption.


BOUNDS RECHECK — WHICH LIMITS REALLY SURVIVE THE RULE FREEDOMS?
------------------------------------------------------------
The existing two-native-tick universal retained-output argument remains
sound under the inspected full rules. It already permits arbitrary
pre-tail density matrices, nonlinear correlations with earlier classical
records, arbitrary intermediate geometry, reset/measurement reuse, and
any legal affine postselection/Pauli frame. It does not assume an ideal
codeword before the extracted terminal noise.

The reasoning distinguishes a final output-outputCX (large loss at least
3.333e-4) from final1q/idle or output-discarded-helperCX. Native preparation
and measurement noise covers last-tick first-use/reset cases. Independent
D1(p/5) factors can be extracted even from a penultimate shared D2 channel
with a nonnegative exact CPTP residual, and moved through the final
output-helperCX after tracing the unmeasured helper. Accepted records cannot
observe these final channels; logical Pauli frames commute with them.

The resulting local channel is qeff=p-16p^2/75 on all7 outputs. Recomputed
exact rational certificate at p=.001:
  qeff =9373/9375000
  minimum loss over arbitrary pre-tail states =
  .00001162029638920668820439987200204524249962631837998654273939265
  certified rounded lower bound =1.162e-5.

bound_recheck.json records the rational CPTP coefficients and all-sector
terminal optimization. No escape from this bound was found. The c1
counterexample does not challenge it: it concerns a different assertion.

Scope classification:
  7.445e-6 last-tick-only number: valid but weaker optimistic lower bound;
    it omits a necessary prior noise factor and is not an achievable circuit.
  1.162e-5: current universal true-expectation retained-output bound under
    STANDARD; it does not bound a selected finite Monte Carlo draw.
  2.567e-5: direct-readout/one-final-idle architecture result; not universal.
    Designs outside that ending require actual noisy construction/pricing.
  3.55e-5: best known grid-free constructed reference in the cited snapshot;
    a performance result, not a lower-bound theorem and not an executable
    6x6-grid guarantee.
  finite-lifetime/static-layout infeasibility: applies only to the encoded
    SAT/ILP family, not to arbitrary measurement instruments or moving code.
  p/30 harmful-Pauli quantum: false, counterexamples above.

Output-code permutations are a genuine placement freedom, but relabeling an
already-built circuit by a Steane code automorphism cannot alone lower its
expected score: it permutes the uniform-noise distribution and equivariant
Hamming decoder. A nonautomorphism requires a new ideal-target proof. Do
not count an invariant relabeling as a fresh physical optimization.

Similarly, global depolarizing-covariant Clifford conjugation with unchanged
preparation, measurement, acceptance and target contracts can be a score
equivalence, not a gain. Partial gauges change the noisy instrument and must
be priced; review6 already found a small physical SX gain plus slow sampling.


STATISTICAL WORDING CORRECTION
-----------------------------
GOAL's term 'True mean' is defined as a3-seed mean or seed-reordering mean.
Those are estimates of the expectation, not the exact expectation itself.
Use distinct fields: independent all-orders expected loss; official seed0
score and SEM; sample mean and its uncertainty. Selecting the minimum of
many seed-reorder results changes a chosen official sample, not the physical
expected loss. The user requested robust optimization claims, so do not
present seed selection as a true-error reduction.
