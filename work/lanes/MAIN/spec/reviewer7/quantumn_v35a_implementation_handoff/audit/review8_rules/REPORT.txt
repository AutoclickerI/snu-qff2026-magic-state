REMAINING ACCEPTANCE BUDGET
---------------------------

The official hard floor is0.5 (CONSTRAINTS14-15; official problem§1.6).
The current0.5005 working margin is a team choice, not a contest rule.
At reported A=0.50124, the absolute remaining rejection probability is:
  to official0.5:0.00124;
  to team0.5005:0.00074.

If a proposed independent selector multiplies acceptance by s, it must
satisfy s>=0.5/0.50124=0.9975261351847418 (hard floor), or
s>=0.5005/0.50124=0.9985236613199264 (team margin).
Thus only0.247386% of currently accepted probability can be additionally
rejected at the hard floor, or0.147634% at the team's working margin.
This arithmetic does NOT imply that correlated guards have a fixed
multiplicative price; their joint acceptance must be computed exactly.

An appended fair branch, the review7 biased branch with ideal A0.85355,
or the legal nonlinear AND selector with ideal A0.75 would all fail the
current budget if used independently. Their value is legal design freedom,
not an add-on to v35a. A useful construction must recover false rejections,
replace existing checks, or operate on a more accepting architecture.

PLAN6/21 reports ENDSTATE R38 at loss5.614e-5,A~0.587. That branch can
in principle keep an additional selector with probability>=0.85178876,
before accounting for correlated physical noise. This is a material
architectural option: worse present loss does not mean a worse constrained
optimum after spending acceptance. Its full frontier needs evaluation;
no gain from any particular composition is guaranteed.

2. FINDINGS95 says the terminal release chain permits no legal schedule
   belowR45 for the listed FRESH prefix/resource attempts. FINDINGS94
   immediately reports a legal R44 using reoriented A. The old closure
   therefore applies only to its former fixed construction model. It is
   not a contest restriction or a lower bound for reoriented instruments.

3. PLAN11 still allocates loss from v23h_c2c9. FINDINGS92 allocates v30g.
   Neither ledger quantifies v35a after A decoding and storage guards.
   In particular, an old A-danger metric can rank a fault as harmful even
   when the new logical-X flag rejects it. Recompute the accepted joint
   fault/record kernel before choosing another encoder or check budget.
   The objective agent owns this calculation.

4. PLAN63 rejects any c1!=0 via a universal p/30 per harmful two-qubit
   Pauli. c1=0 is not an official validity rule. The leading contribution
   for fault f is (p/15)*a_f*ell_f/A0, where a_f is its acceptance, ell_f
   its conditional decoded loss, and A0 ideal acceptance. Substitution
   a_f/A0=1 and ell_f>=1/2 needs a family-specific proof. The review7 audit
   produced no winning nonzero-c1 circuit and no global counterexample
   theorem. Do not turn this caveat into a claim that admitting c1!=0 will
   improve v35a. It identifies an overbroad pruning assertion only.

5. The stationary B geometry obstruction (FINDINGS86-87) remains valid
   for its explicitly stated model: a connected7-cell B with two distinct
   outside partners per cell has at most13 of the required14. It does
   not exclude preparation using measured helpers, moving B before its
   check, changing the instrument, or making the effective resource graph
   through local Clifford operations and measurements. The direct odd-
   cycle obstruction (FINDINGS129) likewise remains correct for direct
   interaction-graph embedding.

RULE-ENABLED DIRECTIONS THAT REMAIN MEANINGFULLY DISTINCT
-------------------------------------------------------

A. Optimize the acceptance instrument, not only which detector rows to drop.
   Replace enforced parity subspaces jointly with physical checks and
   logical frame choice. Invertible basis changes are score-identical;
   XORing an enforced check into a frame is also identical on accepted
   shots. To gain, change the accepted row space, collect genuinely new
   information, or synthesize a different measured instrument. At v35a's
   acceptance boundary, prioritize selective recovery of low-risk rejected
   syndromes that finances more valuable protection. First compute an
   oracle bound for the current measured records. If it leaves no useful
   headroom, do not build a physical nonlinear decoder on those records.

B. Physically computed Boolean acceptance is legal beyond affine comments.
   API DETECTOR/POSTSELECT/FRAME expressions remain affine, but allowed
   gates can compute a Boolean function into a fresh measured bit. Review7
   provides an actual full-target official PASS witness: nearest-neighbour
   8CX+7T/TDG CCX on helpers33-34-35 computes AND of two fair classical
   measurement bits; POSTSELECT AND=0 has ideal acceptance3/4. The three-
   element accepted set is not an affine subset of the two source bits.
   This establishes legal expressivity, not competitive performance.
   Actual syndrome applications must keep/reconstruct record bits, route
   them, satisfy the global commuting-T class, and pay all13 helper ticks
   and gate noise. They must recover enough acceptance/loss to justify it.
   This is distinct from a free classical lookup table, which the interface
   does not supply. See FOLLOWUP_NONLINEAR.txt and its8CX witness.

C. Effective graph resources need not embed as direct gate graphs.
   Review7 explicitly constructs grid star7--{1,6,8}, then Y-measures7.
   Fixed Sdagger on the leaves yields the triangle graph state up to
   (Z1Z6Z8)^m. Both branches have probability1/2 and corrected fidelity
   1 within floating error. All three physical CX are grid-adjacent;
   native depth7; official parser and class pass. This proves a non-
   bipartite effective graph can arise from the bipartite hardware via
   local complementations and measurement. It removes a direct-embedding
   obstruction from the synthesis model, not the resource/area/noise
   problem. The witness is a four-qubit identity, not a full seven-output
   target or a new FT construction. Byproducts must be propagated through
   the eventual instrument into legal logical frames/detectors.
   See FOLLOWUP_GRAPH_MINOR.txt and grid_star_to_triangle.*.

D. Remove evaluator restrictions from the candidate representation.
   AGENTS requires global-used-wire barriers and no FRAME/POSTSELECT only
   for the adopted sparse evaluator; it explicitly allows other methods
   outside that scope. Official ASAP scheduling accepts partial barriers,
   affine logical frames, biased postselection, reused qubits and differing
   lifetimes. A useful next compiler can evaluate these legal candidates
   without converting their intended schedule into a global barrier grid.
   This increases search reach; speedup itself is not a physical loss gain.
   Preserve native schedule/noise exactly and verify against official
   semantics on the particular new family.

E. Use the acceptance frontier across architectures.
   v35a's strong present score and tiny headroom should not become a fixed
   starting-point constraint. Retain nondominated (loss, acceptance, R,
   runtime, geometry) solutions such as R38 A~0.587. Spend acceptance where
   the combined instrument removes the most current accepted risk. This
   broad direction is already ENDSTATE/ST2 work; freshness requires an
   exact joint objective and an additional instrument/geometry variable,
   not simply repeating 'knapsack'.

None of A-E is a guaranteed loss reduction or proof of sub1e-5. B and C
have rigorous legal/algebraic witnesses; performance requires full build.
The current universal-bound audit is owned by the parent/objective lane.
Do not confuse a loose below1e-5 relaxation with an attainable circuit.
