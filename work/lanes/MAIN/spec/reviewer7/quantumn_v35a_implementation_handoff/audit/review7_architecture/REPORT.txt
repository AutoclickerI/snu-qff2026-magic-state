3. GENERALIZE DESTRUCTIVE READOUT, WITH A PROVED LATENCY BOUNDARY
---------------------------------------------------------------
The useful free parameter is the Clifford measurement instrument at the
end of an ancilla block, not only its preparation circuit. Current A's
all-Z readout is not required by the rules. The two-CX result demonstrates
that existingZsyndrome information and additionallogicalX information can
be jointly extracted in the same final depth.

Next search contract:
  Preserve required oldZ syndromes and ideal output;
  synthesize localCNOT/measurement-basis networks exposing additional
  deterministicXstabilizers/logicalX;
  optimize the complete preparation-to-readout window and noise, rather
  than attaching a separately prepared flag after a fixed encoder.

Do not assume more parallel terminal gates immediately give another flag.
The threeA Z-checks have the seven distinct nonzeroGF(2)^3columns. A CNOT
changes columnc tocolumnc XORcolumnt. To read a wire inX while retaining
all threeZchecks, its transformed column must vanish. Exhaustive networks
of0,1,2,3 unrestrictedCNOTs produce atmost0,0,1,1 zero columns. Two zeros
require at least4CNOTs. More strongly, a first layer containing atmostone
CNOT followed byany disjointmatching has atmostone zero column (56,029
cases checked). That matches the fixed current prep/extraction boundary.

Thus another independentX readout requires changing that boundary, using
a larger measurement instrument, or accepting more latency. It cannot
come merely from adding disjoint gates to the last layer. This is a bounded
CSS/destructive-single-qubit-measurement result, not a global no-go theorem.

4. ENCODED RESOURCE FLAGS AND POST-USE VERIFICATION: PRECISE OPEN DOMAINS
------------------------------------------------------------------------
These are research extensions with existing algebraic support, not new
measured improvements in this turn.

A. Transient encoded-resource flags across A and B/C.
The rule does not require separate intermediateA,B,C states. A flag wire
can be part of an encoded resource; individualpurity is unnecessary if
the accumulated error is read through a deterministic encoded parity.
ForF=CX(f,a), U=CX(u,a), F U F=U, while aZ_a fault in the window acquiresZ_f.
This exact identity was certified inreview5_architecture/encoded_flag_certificate.
The new synthesis domain allows transient cross-block entanglement and
requires only the final accepted instrument contract. Currentcrossedges
A14--B13 andA16--C17 provide concrete locations, but their busy schedules
and changing flag operators must be solved jointly. A static echo insertion
without conjugating through the resource encoder is invalid.
First experiment: choose the highest remaining A-fault mechanism after
the new partialdecoder, add its flagged interaction to a joint synthesizer,
prove the complete input/outputdetector contract, and price all noise.
Reject a purported gain if it adds critical-path exposure that cancels it.

7. REOPEN ENCODER SEARCH UNDER THE NEW TERMINAL READOUT CONTRACT
--------------------------------------------------------------
This is a concrete physical synthesis domain, distinct from merely repeating
the old encoder search with its old scalar danger objective. The current
two-CX A decoder supplies a logical-X flag on code support {2,3,6}, physical
cells {8,9,15}, while retaining the original Z syndromes.

The self-contained, exact Pauli certificate enumerates all 21 weight-two Z
pairs on A. Twelve flip the new flag, and nine do not. A pair {i,j} has the
phase class Z_L Z_k modulo a stabilizer, where k=((i+1) XOR (j+1))-1. The new
flag is blind exactly when k belongs to {2,3,6}; pure logical Z is detected.
This is local phase-readout classification, not an assertion that every pair
was accepted by the full old circuit.
