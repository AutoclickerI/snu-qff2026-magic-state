Main finding
------------
Most items in current CONSTRAINTS are faithfully grounded. The larger
restriction is the family's representation: fixed A/B/C roles and endpoint
contracts, a particular prefix/check decomposition, fixed output lifetimes,
fast-evaluator support, and selected fault predicates. Some are already
recognized in PLAN. They should not be presented as newly discovered bans
or as things the team never considered.

The official optimization problem is finite-p conditional decoded loss,
subject to correctness, class, physical grid, noisy acceptance, size, and
server runtime. It does not demand a particular preparation algorithm,
three seven-qubit ancilla blocks, a permanently Steane-encoded trajectory,
zero logical frame, ideal acceptance one, literal T-depth one, minimum
CX count, maximum200CX, or a separate fault-tolerance theorem.

RIGOROUS RULE DISTINCTIONS
-------------------------

1. OUTPUT LOCATION AND LIFETIME ARE FREE; OUTPUT STATE/ORDER ARE NOT.
P112-118 and P360-365 permit any seven distinct final physical sites in an
ordered data_qubits list. Moving/teleporting the logical information,
changing which wires become outputs, temporary coding, and recycled
ancillas are allowed if all actual gates remain legal. The current prefix
footprint, data cell list, final-round timestamp and A/B/C partition are
design choices. Correctness applies at the final outputs, after a logical
Pauli frame but BEFORE the judge's perfect syndrome correction (P304-307).

Do not propose a free gain by merely permuting data_qubits: a non-code
automorphism generally fails correctness, while a Steane/Fano automorphism
also permutes the Hamming decoder covariantly. A pure automorphism relabel
does not change expected decoded loss; an actual circuit/layout change is
needed. This is an algebraic observation, not a new measured speedup.

2. JOINT RESOURCE INSTRUMENTS ARE LEGAL.
There is no rule requiring separate pure A/B/C states, independent
encoders, named resources, a fixed syndrome order, transversal final
extraction, or separate preparation/verification/coupling stages. B/C can
be entangled, outputs can be transferred, and ancillas need not be
uncomputed to |0> merely for cleanliness. The accepted output must still
be the exact target; ideal purity therefore enforces its decoupling from
unobserved ancillas. Noise and record correlations must be evaluated as
an instrument, not erased by assuming independent endpoint states.
The current correlated rank-three B/C resource already uses some of this
freedom. A proposal must identify the newly relaxed endpoint predicate.

3. QUANTUM FEEDFORWARD IS FORBIDDEN, LOGICAL PAULI FRAME IS EXPLICITLY LEGAL.
P143 prohibits if/conditional quantum instructions. P158-169 and P274-309
allow fX and fZ to be affine XORs of measurement records, including constants.
The frame is interpreted classically and carries no added physical Pauli
gate channel. It is a logical X/Z correction only; it cannot declare an
arbitrary physical correction, nonlinear Boolean decoder, or conditional S_L.
One must declare BOTH FRAME_X and FRAME_Z, or neither. Automatically found
frames need not be the only valid choices. Two ideal-equivalent frame
records may differ under noise (P296-299).

Important invariant: adding a parity already enforced by the acceptance
constraints to a frame cannot improve accepted-shot loss. That parity has
the same fixed value on every accepted noisy shot. Frame search needs
record information outside those enforced relations, a changed acceptance
subspace, or an altered physical instrument.

4. RANDOM IDEAL POSTSELECTION IS LEGAL, WITH THE SAME NOISY ACCEPTANCE FLOOR.
P167, P248-259 and exact.py216-246 permit random POSTSELECT branches and
DETECTORs deterministic only within selected branches. Ideal acceptance
does not have to equal1. Declared conditions are affine parity equalities;
arbitrary OR, threshold, lookup-table acceptance and adaptive retries are
not in the submitted interface.

A fair stabilizer branch costs a factor1/2. Adding an independent fair
branch to an existing A~.5 design clearly fails. But all postselection is
not necessarily a half: a legal H-T-X measurement has noiseless P(0)=
cos^2(pi/8)=0.8535533905932737. The full-target witness in this directory
passes all four official gates with this biased selector, noisy A=.8518
at10000shots. A conditional detector on the selected bit also passes.
These are legality witnesses, not lower-loss circuits. A useful heralded
design must outperform the best circuit after paying ALL acceptance,
noise, geometry and class costs.

5. THE DETECTOR SET IS A CHOICE OF PARITY SUBSPACE, NOT JUST A ROW SUBSET.
P158,166,250-255 allow deterministic XORs, including ideal parity1. One
may replace two checks d1=0,d2=0 by d1 XOR d2=0, which accepts both00 and11;
this is a weaker but legal condition. An invertible basis change of the
same full row space changes neither acceptance nor loss. A gain requires
a different row space (or different measurements), priced at full noise.
Past DET/BUDGET/KNAP searches already tested subsets and small subspaces
on older bases; a new candidate must use a newly relevant resource and
jointly optimize protection and accepted syndrome space rather than repeat
the same drop-only search.

6. FULL-GRID BARRIERS ARE NOT REQUIRED.
P235-243 and noise.py54-88 use ASAP scheduling with barrier groups of any
subset. No TICK text can override that schedule. Before first use, before
reset, and after a last measurement, the listed idle charges are absent.
The selected schedule can therefore use different physical lifetimes and
partial synchronization. This does NOT imply deleting all barriers helps:
earlier preparation can expose an ancilla longer, while a global barrier
can intentionally delay its first use. Use native scheduling and exposure
as variables, not line-number depth.

The repository AGENTS describes the adopted sparse evaluator as requiring
barriers over all used qubits and no FRAME/POSTSELECT. These are evaluator
scope limits, not contest rules. AGENTS itself says use other evaluators
outside that scope. Treat unsupported candidates as 'needs evaluator',
not 'illegal'. Review6's extended frontend is also not a general official
frontend; class and correctness still require the judge.

7. RESET AND MEASUREMENT REUSE ARE REAL FREEDOMS, WITH DIFFERENT NOISE.
P116-117 and145-148 permit midcircuit reset and h-measure-h as a native X
readout leaving its observed |+> or |-> state. Reset+h folds into native
RX. These use one native step; folded H carries no extra gate noise.
General H, S and SX gates remain noisy. Measurement flips and post-M
depolarization still exist. A random measurement-prepared state needs its
record byproduct tracked. It is not a free known |+> reset, and resetting
instead can change both finite-noise loss and simulator runtime.

The 36-qubit limit is a simultaneous physical-chip limit, not a total
number of ancilla lifetimes: reused physical sites are allowed. The1000
measure+reset cap and simulator cost still apply.

8. T CLASS IS A COMMUTATION CONSTRAINT, NOT LITERAL ONE-TICK T DEPTH.
P205-213 describes one mutually commuting Pauli-rotation layer after
Clifford propagation. Temporally separated T gates can satisfy it. The
toy H0,H1,T0,CX01,T1,CX01 passes xtim class; H0,T0,H0,T0,H0 is rejected.
No required15T budget is stated. Nevertheless arbitrary multistage
distillation is not automatically admissible. Check propagated axes and
actual xtim class early. Official exact.py has MAX_RANK22 as a further
implementation capacity limit; it is not the old family's X-rank5.

9. ALL LISTED SINGLE-QUBIT GATES ARE LEGAL AND NOISY.
P135 lists S/Sdg/SX/SXdg as well as H/T/Tdg/Paulis. Native SX and SXdg
conversion with their D1 channels is confirmed by the witness. A generator
restricted to H/CX/T is narrower than the contest gate set. The prior
review6 A+SX runtime result remains a practical limit on that tested
family, not a rule against SX.

qasm.GATES and exact.ROTATIONS contain legacy CZ/CS/CH/SWAP handling.
STANDARD.gates filters those out. The explicit CZ native probe fails
format. Their presence in the library is NOT permission to submit them.
They may only be synthesized from permitted operations, paying actual
gate/time/noise costs and passing class.

10. NO200CX CAP; FEWER CX IS A TIEBREAKER.
P345-348 and367-373 specify200000bytes,5000QASM statements,
1000measure+reset and1hour per submission. There is no200CX ceiling.
Primary ranking is finite-p1-F to3 significant figures; then fewer2q
gates and earlier submission. The current repository already contains
>200CX designs, so this should be a guardrail, not an accusation that the
team has imposed a nonexistent cap.

The team's20s-per-million promotion gate is a conservative runtime
predictor for the1hour server limit. It is not itself an official rule.
A different runtime class could be considered if actual whole-run
measurement demonstrates fit in the server budget; short-run linear
extrapolation and the CPU ratio alone are not a guarantee. Existing SX
variants have a measured execution problem, so merely lifting the local
gate would not establish feasibility.

11. c1=0 IS A DIAGNOSTIC, NOT AN ADMISSIBILITY GATE.
P553-564 explicitly says no particular algorithm or fault-tolerance
condition is separately required; P586-591 distinguishes c1 from the
finite-p official score. GOAL already states this correctly.

PLAN's blanket rejection that one harmful2q Pauli necessarily costs
>=p/15 *1/2=3.3e-5 needs family-specific assumptions. Generally a fault f
with weightp/15 contributes (p/15)*a_f*ell_f/A0 at first order, where a_f
is its accepted probability, ell_f its conditional decoded loss, and A0
ideal acceptance. Taking a_f/A0=1 and ell_f>=1/2 is an extra assertion;
random accepted branches and propagated non-Pauli errors require care.
This audit has not produced a winning nonzero-c1 circuit, nor a full-class
counterexample to every possible stronger theorem. Reopen the pruning
rule only with an actual family-specific fault/branch calculation, not
by assuming harmful singles will be cheap.

12. THE FREE FINAL CORRECTION IS PART OF THE SCORE, NOT AN EXTRA GADGET.
P304-307 requires exact target before it on ideal accepted runs. Under
noise, P316-338 and check._sample correct output syndrome without noise;
those six judge measurements cannot reject a shot or be referenced as
participant records. Optimize decoded risk and error correlations, not
raw output syndrome rate. This does not permit ending noiselessly in a
wrong syndrome or attaching an uncharged physical correction.

13. STATISTICAL REDRAWS ARE NOT A PHYSICAL IMPROVEMENT.
P343-354 fixes seed0 and1e8shots with1e6chunks seeded0..99. The public
rule does not require a mean over3seeds. The user repository's3-seed/re-draw
means are estimators, not the exact expectation. Simultaneous gate
reordering may leave the quantum noise channel unchanged while changing
a finite MC realization. It cannot lower expected loss by itself. Report
model gains and official seed-key changes separately. Public text silence
on additional variants should not be promoted to organizer approval of
every conceivable scoring tactic. No seed-selection scan was performed.

Official micro-witness results
-----------------------------
rule_witnesses.py uses unchanged tprep.check and official dependencies.
Four FULL-TARGET10k-shot cases all pass four gates:
  organizer_injection_control: ideal A1; noisy A1.
  biased_noiseless_postselection: ideal A.8535533905932737; noisy A.8518.
  conditional_detector: same acceptance; conditionally deterministic bit.
  constant_logical_X_frame: ideal A1; noisy A1.
Scores of these intentionally simple injection circuits are poor and are
not optimization candidates. Native-only probes cover RX, MX reuse, SX,
partial/global barriers, separated commutingT, noncommutingT rejection,
legacyCZ rejection and lifetime idle exemptions. Their scope is not
confused with target-state validation. Complete results and QASMs are
stored beside this report; no performance claim is made from them.

Best consequences for the parent's10-candidate review
----------------------------------------------------
Prioritize expansions with a concrete changed feasible set:
  - terminal resource decoding / extra commuting information on discarded
    blocks (already a validated gain; extend with exact schedule/resource budgets);
  - joint acceptance-subspace/protection/frame optimization on the new
    binding-acceptance base, beyond dropping detector rows independently;
  - conditional-instrument endpoint synthesis with measured Pauli byproducts;
  - lifetime/partial-barrier and moving-output geometry co-design;
  - biased heralding only in a redesigned high-acceptance architecture;
  - physical Clifford gauge enumeration with measured simulator feasibility;
  - commuting-phase parity-network re-synthesis without preserving the old
    prefix's internal state or its chosen block partition.

These are consequences of documented freedoms, not universal performance
guarantees. The actual rule audit does not establish a way below1e-5.
