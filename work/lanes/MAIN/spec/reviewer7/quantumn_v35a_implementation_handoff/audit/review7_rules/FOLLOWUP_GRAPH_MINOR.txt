FOLLOW-UP — A BIPARTITE GRID RESOURCE CAN HAVE A NONBIPARTITE GRAPH ENDPOINT
========================================================================

Verified endpoint identity, not a complete contest candidate.

Physical resource: a4-qubit star on the6x6 grid.
  center7; leaves1,6,8; edges7--1,7--6,7--8.
Prepare|+> on all four and applyCZ on each star edge, decomposed into the
allowed H-CX-H. Measure center7 inY usingSdg,H,measure.

For measurement bitm in{0,1}, the normalized raw leaf state is
  S_1 S_6 S_8 (Z_1 Z_6 Z_8)^m |K3>,
up to branch-dependent global phase, where|K3> is the triangle graph state
on the ordered leaves[1,6,8]. Each outcome has probability1/2.
Apply the fixed Sdg to each leaf to obtain
  (Z_1 Z_6 Z_8)^m |K3>.
Only this fixed correction is emitted; the outcome-dependent Pauli is
retained as a symbolic byproduct for downstream legal record handling.

Checks performed:
  - Statevector on4qubits; both branches have probability.5 within roundoff.
  - After fixedSdg and the symbolicZ byproduct, fidelity with|K3> is
    .9999999999999996 for both outcomes.
  - Triangle stabilizers XZZ,ZXZ,ZZX all have expectation+1.
  - Actual QASM uses3nearest-neighborCX, no nativeCZ or Y measurement.
  - Official parser passes; native depth7; xtim class passes.

The4-node physical star is bipartite. Its three-leaf graph-state endpoint
after local Clifford and measurement is nonbipartite. Thus a no-go theorem
for directly embedding an interaction triangle in the square grid does
not exclude measurement-based graph-state/resource synthesis. The direct
embedding theorem remains correct within its own model.

All threeCX, physical1q gates, preparations, measurement and idles carry
the official noise. A graph-minor identity is not a finite-noise fidelity
claim, a fault-tolerance certificate, or a proof that the resource improves
the current seven-qubit target circuit. Compile the local Cliffords and
record-dependent Paulis into the whole instrument; price its schedule and
its correlated faults. An arbitrary physical Pauli byproduct cannot be
declared directly as a logical7-qubit frame without this propagation.
