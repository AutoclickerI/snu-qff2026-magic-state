1. EXECUTIVE FINDINGS
=====================

The highest-priority physical search is A-encoder synthesis under the NEW
logical-X readout flag. The old encoder objective penalizes errors that the
new flag can reject. Consequently, exhaustive optimization of that old
objective does not establish optimality of the new complete instrument.
Exact examples show that formerly tied encoder profiles have different
remaining risks after the flag. This is a concrete changed objective, not
a suggestion to repeat the same search unchanged.

Second, v35a has very little acceptance slack. Complete physical guard and
readout replacements expose useful budget trades. Removing the q20 X guard
returns approximately 0.008352 acceptance for a loss penalty of 2.6059e-7.
Exchanging two C readout copies for one readout CX returns 0.002413 acceptance
for a loss penalty of 1.0301e-7. Neither is a standalone improvement. They
are measured starting points for spending acceptance on a better check.

Third, a blanket c1!=0 rejection based on a supposed minimum p/30 harmful
fault contribution is false. An exact legal witness has a p/120 individual
contribution. The witness's total circuit is uncompetitive; it invalidates
the universal pruning argument, not the current good circuit.

Fourth, actual legal witnesses establish two broader design spaces:
  - Nonlinear acceptance on original records can be realized by computing a
    Boolean function using allowed quantum gates and selecting its output.
  - Measurement can produce effective non-bipartite graph resources on the
    bipartite physical grid. A star-to-triangle construction is certified.

No new circuit in this review directly beats v35a. Five complete guard
removals, two C readout variants, and a tracked-initialization policy were
priced. The results, including failures, are retained. The proposed methods
have different evidence levels: completed measurements, exact local proofs,
legal expressiveness witnesses, or explicitly bounded research hypotheses.
No percentage gain is promised for an unbuilt whole circuit.

4. ACTUAL RULES AND EXTRA DESIGN ASSUMPTIONS
==========================================

Physical and submission constraints remain binding. The chip has 36 sites
on a 6x6 orthogonal nearest-neighbor grid. Native two-qubit gates are CX.
Allowed one-qubit operations include id,x,y,z,h,s,sdg,sx,sxdg,t,tdg; reset,
measurement and barrier are allowed. Native three-qubit gates, arbitrary
rotations, custom gate definitions and conditional quantum instructions are
not allowed. A function such as CCX may nevertheless be synthesized from
permitted operations if the complete circuit passes format and xtim class.

The output is an ordered set of seven distinct physical sites. It must be
the exact target on ideal accepted runs after the permitted logical Pauli
frame and BEFORE the judge's final ideal syndrome correction. Under noise,
that final correction contributes to the scoring definition; its syndrome
bits are not participant records and cannot be used for rejection.

Noisy acceptance must satisfy the official0.5 gate at p=.001. Other actual
limits include200,000bytes,5,000statements,1,000measure+reset operations and
one hour of server runtime. CX count is a tiebreaker after three-significant-
figure score comparison, followed by submission time. There is no200CX cap.

The following are not universal contest requirements:
  - Initial and final data wires being identical.
  - Three independent pure A/B/C blocks at every intermediate boundary.
  - A fixed Steane encoding throughout the trajectory.
  - A global preparation/verification/extraction stage boundary or a single R.
  - A0=1, a zero logical frame, c1=0, or a particular preparation algorithm.
  - All T gates appearing in one literal scheduling tick.
  - Full-grid barriers or one fixed lifetime per resource wire.

Affine logical FRAME_X and FRAME_Z are legal; declare both or neither.
They do not authorize arbitrary physical corrections or conditional S.
POSTSELECT may have random ideal branches, and DETECTORs may be deterministic
only within the selected branches. The annotation interface uses affine
parity conditions; nonlinear functions can instead be computed physically.

Class restrictions concern commuting Pauli rotation axes after Clifford
propagation and actual simulator processing. The official exact engine has
implementation capacity limits as well. Presence of legacy CZ/SWAP handlers
inside a library does not override the STANDARD allowed-gate filter.

Native ASAP scheduling determines noise. Partial barriers are legal. Noise
exemptions before first use, before reset and after a final measurement make
lifetime a design variable. Deleting all barriers can still worsen loss by
preparing resources too early. Only documented RX/MX folding makes the
associated H operations free; general H/S/SX operations are noisy.

The team's0.5005 acceptance margin and20seconds-per-million promotion gate
are internal choices, not the literal official thresholds. They can be
revisited with evidence; doing so alone does not establish feasible runtime
or a beneficial circuit. Existing slow SX families remain measured failures
unless a new equivalent representation actually runs fast.

At v35a's expected A, an independent extra selector may reject only
1-0.5/A =0.0024677723 of current accepted events, approximately0.2468%.
A fair branch or a0.853553 biased branch cannot simply be appended. A new
heralded design must recover acceptance, replace checks, or start from a
different high-acceptance architecture.

Evidence: audit/review7_rules/rules_matrix.json and FOLLOWUP_NONLINEAR.txt;
audit/review8_rules/REPORT.txt; audit/review7_rules/official/problem.md and the pinned tprep source files.

5. COMMON EVALUATION STANDARD
============================

Use actual circuit bytes and the current native schedule. Charge every
added CX, one-qubit gate, measurement, reset, routing operation and idle.
Preserve record semantics, measured-state reuse and the ordered output list.
Check ideal output stabilizers and target before the judge's correction.
Use exact/algebraic screens where appropriate, but distinguish their scope
from floating calculations and from full official validation.

Rank supported circuit families by complete expected N/A, where N is the
accepted loss mass and A the accepted probability. c1=0 is useful diagnostic
evidence, not a sufficient quality test or a universal feasibility gate.
The tracked-init experiment below is first-order safe yet loses25.6% at the
actual finite p. Low-order loss decompositions can also overlap and should
not be added as independent gains.

The sparse prototype is not a universal official frontend. Its restrictions
on barriers, POSTSELECT, normalization and signs are implementation scope
limits. An unsupported legal candidate needs a correct evaluator, not a
fabricated illegal classification. End finalists with the unchanged official
checker. Respect project-defined compute and promotion gates; avoid repeated
large runs once a candidate is decisively worse in the full model.

METHOD1: FLAG-CONDITIONED A-ENCODER SYNTHESIS
-------------------------------------------
Priority: highest near-term physical search.
Evidence level: exact objective change; no new winning encoder yet.

Let F={2,3,6} be the logical-X flag support in code coordinates. A pure
Z_i Z_j error before extraction flips the flag exactly when its intersection
with F is odd. Of21position pairs,12are detected and9are blind. This is a
classification of error types, not a57% performance prediction.

Published EXENC danger vectors demonstrate a real reranking. In units p/15,
the old reference has10units, of which4are flag-blind. A13CX/depth7 profile
has9old units and3blind units; a13CX/depth8 profile also has9old units but6
blind units. A12CX/depth9 profile has8old units and3blind units. Two formerly
tied paths therefore have a factor-two difference in this local remaining
danger proxy. Whole-circuit effects may change the final ranking.

Current v35a is not the old reference. Its measured local danger vector is
[0,3,4,0,4,0,0,0] in the order(Z_L,Z_LZ_0,...,Z_LZ_6), totaling11units;
the blind component totals4. This was computed with the pinned EXENC score()
function and the actual current encoder layers and input pivots.

First reprice the historical ACHECK paths with the current initialization,
readout and timing. Then rerun synthesis with detector flips, propagated
data Paulis and time exposure retained in the state/path cost. An ideal
endpoint alone does not identify a noisy instrument: old best-path-per-state
pruning can discard a new winner. Keep an appropriate Pareto frontier or
prove dominance for the new objective before pruning.

EXENC explored11,811rank-four subspaces and canonical11-13CX paths. ASAP
schedules were enumerated; alternative layerings were exhaustive only
through depth6. Its reference6.6667e-4 is not the global depth<=8minimum;
the source table includes6.0e-4 at13CX/depth7and8. Neither bare danger nor
old exhaustive bounds establish optimality under the present readout.

Success: lower full finite-p loss at feasible acceptance and official runtime.

EXENC_REPORT.md, ACHECK_REPORT.md, and audit/review8_objective/current_contract_certificate.*.

METHOD3: WEIGHTED FINITE-p FIRST-ORDER RISK BUDGET
-----------------------------------------------
Priority: change the common pruning policy when the current assumption appears.
Evidence level: exact counterexample and a sound alternative bound.

For one D2 Pauli fault f, its first-order contribution is(p/15)a_f ell_f/A0,
not necessarily p/30. a_f is accepted branch probability and ell_f is the
conditional decoded loss. Both matter after non-Clifford propagation and
postselection.

A legal witness in the prior v31s+A family removes detector rows2and15,
corresponding to{c2}and{c15,c17,c19,c20,c24}. For XI after CX7->6, exact
cyclotomic arithmetic gives a_f=1/4,ell_f=1/2, hence p/120=8.3333333e-6.
The witness passes the official validity gates but has bad total loss.
Another witness has accepted conditional loss1/4. Neither is a submission.

Allow a small weighted first-order budget when a candidate eliminates enough
other noise. Evaluate full finite-p loss, rather than treating c1!=0 as
automatic infeasibility. A useful rigorous algebraic early bound is:
  A_upper=P0*A0+A1+(1-P0-P1),
  L_full>=N1/A_upper.
P0/P1 are probabilities of exactly0/1nonidentity effective channels; A1 and
N1 are their accepted mass and accepted loss. Missing loss is nonnegative,
and missing acceptance is at most the omitted probability mass.

Use exact finite-p probabilities for aggregated idle channels. The supplied
implementation uses floating arithmetic; interval/rational evaluation is
required to advertise its numerical pruning decision as fully certified.
The bound immediately rejects the two deliberately bad witnesses while
avoiding the unsupported universal p/30quantization argument.

Evidence: audit/review7_objective/c1_exact_witness.*, finite_p_guard.* and
official witness receipts. No competitive nonzero-c1circuit has been found.

METHOD4: PHYSICALLY COMPUTED NONLINEAR ACCEPTANCE
----------------------------------------------
Priority: wider research space after an optimistic policy oracle finds headroom.
Evidence level: actual full-target legal witness; no low-loss implementation.

Affine annotations do not forbid evaluating a nonlinear Boolean function
with legal quantum gates and selecting its measured output. An8CX+7T/Tdag
Toffoli construction on the grid path33-34-35computes the AND of two fair
bits. Selecting AND=0accepts{00,01,10}, with ideal probability3/4. Together
with the organizer's target preparation, the full circuit passes format,
class, correctness and acceptance. Native CCX itself remains forbidden.

First compute joint record-sector probabilities and corrected logical
moments. Give an optimistic oracle arbitrary selection and best logical
Pauli per sector. Only synthesize a physical Boolean classifier if its
headroom exceeds the likely circuit cost. Retaining syndrome information,
copying records into physical systems, reversible computation, routing and
measurement all cost noise and time. The global commuting-T class may fail
when the helper is coupled to the actual magic-state circuit.

A biased postselection witness also passes with idealcos²(pi/8)=0.853553...
acceptance. This refutes a blanket factor-of-two assumption, but neither a
3/4nor0.85355independent selector fits v35a's present acceptance budget.
Use replacement/recovered budget or a different high-acceptance structure.
Do not repeat the already negative fixed-C-only or narrow two-record oracle.

METHOD5: MEASUREMENT-BASED GRAPH-RESOURCE SYNTHESIS
------------------------------------------------
Priority: concrete broader preparation family with a legal local witness.
Evidence level: exact structural identity and numerical branch verification.

A bipartite physical grid cannot directly contain a triangle of gate edges.
It can produce a triangle graph state after helper measurement. Prepare a
star centered at7with leaves1,6,8using three CZs synthesized asH-CX-H.
Measure the center in Y. Both outcomes yield the triangle graph state after
fixed local Clifford corrections and an outcome-dependent Pauli byproduct.

The construction uses3nearest-neighbor CXs and7native ticks. Each branch
has probability1/2; corrected fidelity is1to floating precision. Official
format and xtim class pass. This four-qubit example does not constitute
seven-qubit target preparation or a fault-tolerance/performance result.

Synthesize the required A/B/C stabilizer correlations as a measured resource,
not only as a directly embedded interaction graph. Spare sites can be
measured away. Track byproducts into allowed logical frames/detectors; a
conditional Clifford correction is not automatically free or legal. Price
local gates, noisy measurements, correlated helper faults and timing.
Direct-embedding no-go results remain correct within their old model.

Evidence: audit/review7_rules/graph_vertex_minor.py, grid_star_to_triangle.*,
FOLLOWUP_GRAPH_MINOR.txt. This supplies the missing local legal witness

METHOD6: TRANSIENT FLAGS USING ENCODED RESOURCE WIRES
--------------------------------------------------
Priority: targeted joint-instrument research.
Evidence level: exact interaction identity; whole-grid winner not built.

A flag wire need not be an independently pure qubit if a later deterministic
encoded parity reads the error trace. ForF=CX(f,a),U=CX(u,a),FUF=U. A Za
fault inside the echo gains a Zf trace. The ideal identity does not assume
a pure individual flag state.

Actual cross-edges A14-B13 and A16-C17provide starting locations. Choose a
remaining v35a error mechanism and synthesize a joint A/B/C instrument that
makes its trace observable. Track the flag operator through the resource
encoder; inserting the static echo around a changing encoded resource does
not preserve the simple identity automatically.

Current B/C resources are already correlated. The new freedom is transient
cross-block entanglement during preparation and a final instrument contract
in place of separate intermediate pure-state endpoints. This differs from
the old borrowed-clean-cell schedules that delayed preparation and lost.
Reject constructions whose new data exposure, helper faults or acceptance
cost outweigh the recovered information.

Evidence: audit/review5_architecture/encoded_flag_certificate.* and actual v35a
roles/schedules. No predicted whole-circuit gain is assigned.

METHOD7: C-FREE POST-USE VERIFICATION WITH A TERMINAL-LATENCY BUDGET
----------------------------------------------------------------
Priority: architectural research after a concrete timing construction exists.
Evidence level: exact ideal instrument; naive physical realization failed.

Post-use A/B inverse-encoder verification can realize the accepted PZ PX
instrument without a separate C block. The4^7Pauli certificate establishes
the ideal contract and harmless propagation of undetected preparation
Paulis. It does not remove the noise cost of the actual inverse decoder.

The naive full inverse leaves data idling for four ticks and costs roughly
2.1e-4. A diagnostic that artificially leaves only one terminal idle is not
an achievable performance prediction. Removing C alone is not the method.

The open synthesis task is a partial inverse that extracts only required
parities, moves compatible verification through individual extraction
operations, or changes output ownership/lifetimes so verification finishes
without a long tail. Fix a terminal-exposure budget during synthesis rather
than appending checks after an otherwise frozen schedule. Global stage and
block boundaries are not contest requirements.

Evidence: audit/review5_architecture/postuse_kraus_certificate.*, the retained
postuse_decode_r45receipt and explicitly labeled nonphysical diagnostic.
Do not rerun the same failed synchronous full inverse as a fresh idea.

METHOD8: FAULT-TOLERANT VERIFICATION OF THE KNOWN LOGICAL T STATE
--------------------------------------------------------------
Priority: high-risk target-specific instrument research.
Evidence level: current necessary class screen plus a strong counterexample.

The target is one known state, not an identity operation on every logical
input. In the logical convention Y_L=i X_L Z_L=-Y^tensor7,
W_L=(X_L+Y_L)/sqrt(2)=(X^tensor7-Y^tensor7)/sqrt(2) stabilizes |T_L>.
This suggests checking logical-state errors in addition to code syndromes.

On actual v35a, the Clifford-frame pullback of Z_L and all seven weight-three
logical-Zrepresentatives commutes with all15existing Trotation axes. This
necessary screen does not rule out the rotations used by a controlled-W
decomposition. It is not a full gadget, a full xtimclass verdict or a score.

An ideal RAW late projector(I+W_L)/2is unsafe: one correctable physicalX0or
Y0fault produces acceptance1/2and conditional decoded loss1/2. A noiseless
target test alone misses this first-order problem. Weight-three versions
also exhibit it.

Require a syndrome-dressed observable W_dec=sum_s Pi_s C_s^dagger W_L C_s,
or a joint fault-tolerant schedule whose later syndrome checks reject the
dangerous non-code branches and gadget faults. An explicit legal noisy grid
construction and full validation are necessary before assigning a benefit.
This is different from merely replacing injection with a cleaner source.

Evidence: audit/review8_screen/target_specific_class_screen.* and
audit/review7_architecture/target_specific_guard.*.

METHOD9: EXACT LOW-STRATUM INTEGRATION AND CONDITIONAL FAULT SAMPLING
------------------------------------------------------------------
Priority: evaluator work for legal families outside the fast current scope.
Evidence level: numerator-variance bound; no implemented speedup benchmark.

For v35a's539effective modeled channels, P(K=0)=0.4515496720759748,
P(K=1)=0.35963638446217633andP(K>=2)=0.1888139434618481. Kcounts nonidentity
effective channels, including exact aggregated idle channels; it is not a
literal primitive-fault count or a Taylor-order truncation.

Integrate K<=1exactly and sample fromE={K>=2}with correct conditional weights:
  Nhat=N_low+P(E)*mean(n|E), Ahat=A_low+P(E)*mean(a|E).
For the same number m of residual evaluations,
  Var(P(E)*mean(G|E))=P(E)^2*Var(G|E)/m <=P(E)*Var(G)/m.
Thus the numerator estimator's variance is at most approximately18.89%of
the ordinary numerator-estimator variance under those assumptions.

This is not a5.3xwall-clock guarantee or a finite-sample guarantee for N/A.
Include exact-integration cost, conditional-sampling overhead and joint
numerator/acceptance covariance. Never use the unweighted conditional score.
Keep the existing all-orders evaluator/cache where it is already effective.
Final competition-setting verification uses the unchanged official sampler.

Evidence: audit/review8_screen/fault_order_budget.*. A new conditional sampler is
not included; this archive contains the distribution and proof conditions.

7. BOUNDS AND CLOSED DIRECTIONS
==============================

The oldTREPstatement that15other phase forms were unbuilt is stale. TREP2
and FINDINGS record all16grid parity constructions at losses2.3e-4to7.6e-4
and acceptance.35-.40. Repeating the same construction is not a new method.
Current fixed-record policy failures, the late C-edge failure and the naive
full inverse likewise remain closed within their tested domains.

The lower-bound statements must be distinguished:
  - About7.445e-6is a weaker last-tick-only relaxation. A lower bound below a
    target does not construct a circuit achieving that target.
  - L>=1.162e-5is an exact-rationally certified rounded-down retained-output
    bound for true expected decoded loss under the inspected STANDARD model.
  - About2.567e-5belongs to a narrower direct-readout ending, not all circuits.
  - About3.55e-5is a grid-free constructed reference, not a lower-bound theorem.
  - The universal p/30individual harmful-fault claim is false and independent
    of the retained-output bound.

The retained-output proof extracts independent local noise on every output
with q_eff=p-16p²/75. It covers final output-helper CXs, preceding gate/idle/
measurement/reset/first-use preparation channels, arbitrary conditional
pre-tail states and all64syndrome sectors. Final output-output CXs give a
separate larger bound. Earlier records cannot observe the extracted terminal
channels. Logical Pauli frames commute with them. The exact decoder operator
optimization yields1.1620296389206688...e-5; the archived certificate proves
the safely rounded inequality L>=1.162e-5.

Output relocation, earlier physically computed Boolean acceptance, reset
reuse and branch correlations are not counterexamples to that argument.
The bound constrains true expectation, not a selected finite MC draw. A
different official noise/correctness model would require rechecking its
assumptions; an optimistic partial-noise number is not evidence against it.

Evidence: audit/subthreshold_bound/PROOF.txt, terminal_bound.py and rational
certificates; audit/review7_objective/bound_recheck.json.
