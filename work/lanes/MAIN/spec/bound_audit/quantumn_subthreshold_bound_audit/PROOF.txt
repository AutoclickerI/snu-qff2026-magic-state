Two-native-tick retained-output lower bound for this judge
========================================================

Claim: Under the inspected STANDARD model at p=1/1000, every admissible circuit
whose noiseless accepted output is the specified retained 7-qubit Steane T state
has TRUE EXPECTED decoded infidelity at least 0.00001162. This is not a bound
on a finite Monte Carlo realization / selected seed-0 sample.

Assumptions used directly from the judge:
  * Native operations last one scheduling tick and qubit use cannot overlap.
  * Last allowed entangling gate is a CX; single-qubit gates have D1(p) noise.
  * Every CX has D2(p) noise, independent of all other locations.
  * Every waiting output qubit receives D1(p) per tick. Aggregation of consecutive
    idles is exactly equivalent. No noise before first use is required by proof.
  * Non-output quantum systems are discarded; only already-created classical
    records determine acceptance and product-Pauli logical frames.
  * Native reset/measurement destroys a qubit's entanglement on each record
    branch; all seven final Steane qubits must be entangled with the remainder.
  * The final ideal syndrome round CORRECTS every syndrome, without postselecting
    its values; scoring is the actual X/Y target fidelity after that lookup.

Let T be the circuit's last native tick.

1. Final output-output CX case.
------------------------------
If a CX at T touches two retained output qubits, its following D2(p) acts on
those qubits after all participant operations that can observe it. Conditional
acceptance elsewhere cannot depend on this channel. Optimizing the subsequent
judge-decoded target projector over all 128-dimensional input density operators
gives minimum loss 0.000333328885923969... . Exact rational inequalities certify
at least 0.0003333. This already exceeds the desired bound.

The operator optimization is performed in all 64 stabilizer-syndrome sectors,
each a 2D logical space; it does NOT assume a perfect code-space input. All
pairs of Steane qubits are equivalent under code automorphisms; the certificate
nevertheless computes ALL 21 pairs and verifies the bound separately for each.

2. Every remaining final output operation is local or output-helper CX.
------------------------------------------------------------------------
A final output reset/measurement (or an untouched output) cannot satisfy exact
noiseless Steane entanglement. This includes a last measurement EARLIER than T
whose output is never reused: the scheduler skips its subsequent idle noise,
but that qubit stays separable from the retained rest in every measurement
branch. Operations or postselection confined to the rest, and product-Pauli
logical frames, cannot recreate its entanglement. The exact noiseless checker
requires the retained output in the Steane code space and target state, so this
idle-exemption case is inadmissible. The same applies to a last reset at any
earlier time, or a measured/reset output followed only by local 1q operations.
Thus at T every admissible output either idles, has a 1q
unitary, or has a CX with a non-output helper. The helper in that last CX has no
later measurement or gate and is discarded. D2(p)'s exact data marginal after
tracing this helper is D1(4p/5), irrespective of helper entanglement or any
conditioning on disjoint records. One-qubit and idle D1(p) can also factor out
D1(4p/5) with a CPTP residual. Therefore all seven outputs receive independent
terminal D1(4p/5) channels, with all remaining physics absorbed in an arbitrary
conditional pre-channel state.

3. Find one pre-final local noise source for each final-CX output.
----------------------------------------------------------------
For an output having a final CX at T, inspect tick T-1. If it has a unitary or
an idle there, use that channel. If it has a measurement, its explicit
after-measurement D1(p) is available. If it has R/RX, its preparation channel
also factors a D1(p/5): for q=p/5, replace its residual preparation error by

  p' = (p - 2q/3)/(1 - 4q/3) = 13p/(15-4p).

Then D1(q) after this modified noisy reset gives EXACTLY the original reset.
The residual p' is 13/14996 at p=.001 and is a valid probability. This is a
state-preparation channel identity, valid even when the reset discards previous
entanglement. The RX proof is identical in the X basis.

If no idle at T-1 is charged because this qubit is first used at T, STANDARD
initial=1 supplies a noisy |0> just before its final CX. That preparation has
the same decomposition. Thus even the first-use exception is covered without
assuming its feasibility as a correct Steane state.

For outputs with a final 1q gate / idle there is no need to find a prior source:
their terminal D1(p) alone contains the final q_eff channel derived below.

4. Extract independent D1(p/5) from every required pre-final channel.
-------------------------------------------------------------------
Set q=p/5, a=1-4q/3, lambda=1-16p/15. The identity

  D2(p) = [D1(q) tensor D1(q)] composed with R

has a Pauli-diagonal CPTP residual with probabilities

  R_II     = (1 + 6 lambda/a + 9 lambda/a^2)/16,
  R_weight1= (1 + 2 lambda/a - 3 lambda/a^2)/16 for each of 6 Paulis,
  R_weight2= (1 - 2 lambda/a +   lambda/a^2)/16 for each of 9 Paulis.

At p=.001 these are exactly
  224745025/224880016,
  9/224880016,
  14993/224880016,
respectively. They are nonnegative and sum to one with multiplicities 1,6,9.

This handles even a penultimate CX SHARED BY TWO OUTPUTS: both independent
factors are extracted together. For output-helper CX the helper factor is
absorbed into R. For 1q gates / idles / post-measurement channels, D1(p) factors
out D1(q) directly. For reset / first-use channels use Step 3's exact identity.

5. Move these factors to the end without affecting any acceptance record.
------------------------------------------------------------------------
D1(q) commutes with any final single-qubit unitary and with idle noise. Through
a final CX from/to a discarded helper, each output Pauli becomes the same Pauli
on the output times a Pauli on that helper. The latter disappears on partial
trace. Thus the entire D1(q) channel commutes through the final operation after
discarding helpers, even for arbitrary entanglement and conditional states.
Disjoint final measurements commute with these operations and cannot observe
the extracted errors. Product-Pauli logical frames commute with depolarization.

For each final-CX output, combining with its final-tick factor gives

  D1(q_eff), q_eff = p/5 + 4p/5 - (4/3)(p/5)(4p/5)
                  = p - 16p^2/75
                  = 9373/9375000 at p=.001.

Every final 1q/idle output has D1(p), which factors out this q_eff because
q_eff < p. Consequently all seven outputs have independent D1(q_eff), and
all additional noise and measurement correlations remain in a CPTP residual.

6. Optimize the ACTUAL decoder over arbitrary pre-tail states.
------------------------------------------------------------
Let H be the judge's decoded target-fidelity observable on 7 qubits. The accepted
state before the extracted channel may be arbitrary, including residual syndrome,
correlations and deliberate logical prebias. Its best possible final fidelity is

  lambda_max[ D1(q_eff)^{tensor 7}(H) ].

Pauli channels preserve the 64-block stabilizer decomposition. In each block the
observable has eigenvalues [1 +/- sqrt((v_x^2+v_y^2)/2)]/2, where v_x,v_y are exact
signed expectations of the ideal decoder's corrected logical X and Y.

terminal_bound.py enumerates all 4^7 Pauli patterns using rational probabilities,
then ALL 64 initial syndrome sectors. It finds

  minimum loss = 0.00001162029638920668820439987200204524...

and checks, using exact rational integer comparisons in every sector,

  (v_x^2+v_y^2)/2 <= (1 - 2*0.00001162)^2.

Therefore L >= 1.162e-5 is a finite-p numerical certificate backed by the
channel/causality proof, not a leading-order expansion. No assumption about
the preparation algorithm, postselection amount, ancilla quality, or layout
is used. Relaxing grid adjacency cannot evade this particular boundary result.

Interpretation
--------------
The weaker last-tick-only relaxation, D1(.8p) on seven otherwise perfect outputs,
gives loss 7.44545573875e-6 on a perfect T input (7.44545352763e-6 after arbitrary
prebias optimization). It is BELOW1e-5, but it is not an implementable full-circuit
witness: a final-CX output also has a preceding gate/idle/measurement/reset or
first-use preparation noise source. That necessary extra noise raises the bound
above1e-5. The old entanglement-based penultimate reset exclusion is therefore
not needed; final reset/measurement exclusion is still required and immediate.

The repository's 1.116e-5 checked-dummy figure is a different architectural
relaxation that omits the previous output noise. Its label 'any circuit' is
unsupported by that code; the present derivation is an independent argument.
