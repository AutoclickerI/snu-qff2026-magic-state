1. A NEW, STRONGER LOWER BOUND

Under the inspected STANDARD judge and p=1/1000, this study derives

  E[1-F | accepted] >= 2.1892512824770077772...e-5.

An exact rounded-down statement is L >= 2.189e-5.

This is original analysis, independently checked within this study. It is not a
result already published in the repository, an organizer endorsement, or proof
that a complete noisy preparation attains it. The former universal certificate
1.3657850790536654e-5 remains valid, but is substantially loose.

The strengthened proof retains the structure of the penultimate native layer.
Let S contain outputs and the helpers used in CXs at the global final tick T.
Later participant measurements are disjoint from S. Penultimate contacts within
S cannot simply be treated as generic cheap residual noise:

- Output-output contacts have an optimized complete-channel floor 3.3332889e-4.
- An output coupled to a different output's helper, or two same-axis helpers,
  has an optimized floor at least 1.3331555e-4.
- Opposite-axis helper contacts are possible, but their correlated channel
  factors independent propagated axis flips of rate 4p/15, with a positive
  correlated residual channel.
- A contact with an outside partner measured at T factors D1(2p/5) on an output,
  or the propagated axis flip 4p/15 for a helper.
- An output's penultimate contact with its own final helper contributes
  D1(4p/5), in addition to the final CX's D1(4p/5).

The exclusions optimize whole channels over all 64 incoming syndrome sectors;
they are not inferred from a single weight-two fault on a code-space input.
Reset, first-use, postmeasurement, final local non-Clifford gates, unmeasured
outside partners, arbitrary entanglement, postselection and logical frames are
covered in the physical reduction.

Set q=2p/5 and r=4p/15. The remaining independent output factors have four types:
  L: D1(p) composed with D1(q)
  X: D1(4p/5) composed with D1(q) and an X flip of rate r
  Z: D1(4p/5) composed with D1(q) and a Z flip of rate r
  P: D1(4p/5) composed with D1(4p/5).

Exact integer arithmetic covers 4^7 assignments and 64 syndrome sectors each,
through 264 explicitly listed GL(3,2) orbits. The group is checked to preserve
the actual Steane stabilizer rowspace. Seven X-type endings are the unique
minimizing assignment, with the zero incoming syndrome sector.

Independent verification constructs the physical 128x128 decoded-fidelity
observable from Steane CSS codewords, applies Pauli matrices, and diagonalizes
all 264 representative channels plus six excluded/mixed-axis cases. It shares
neither the certificate's moment/convolution routines nor the sparse evaluator.
34,560 eigenvalues agree within 9.55e-15. The optimum differs by 1.82e-15.
A separate physical-case review found no omitted legal case. The full derivation,
certificate and independent implementation are included.

  Current v37g                         36.211237e-6
  Old universal relaxation             13.657851e-6
  New independently checked bound      21.892513e-6
  Current measured-partner class floor 22.742700e-6

v37g is 2.6513 times the old bound, but 1.6540 times the stronger one. The
remaining theoretical mean reduction is at most 39.54%, before charging an
attainable full preparation. A true mean of 2.0e-5 or 1.5e-5 is excluded by the
new bound. Finite-shot official scores are not bounded in this way.

The gap between the measured-partner class floor and the new universal bound is
only .850187e-6. That is 5.94% of the remaining v37g-to-bound headroom. Most of the
available improvement lies above the existing class floor. Escaping that class
alone does not explain a large jump; reducing the extra check, preparation and
waiting costs is essential.

Meaningful full-circuit targets are:
  3.0e-5: 17.15% improvement
  2.8e-5: 22.68% improvement
  2.5e-5: 30.96% improvement
  2.2e-5: 39.25% improvement, extremely close to the new bound.

4. A CONCRETE NEW TERMINAL CONSTRUCTION

Use seven four-qubit tiles, each with data D, unmeasured helper H and checks K,L.
One native 2x2 embedding has H,D in the top row and L,K in the bottom row.

For each tile, the last two layers are:
  T-1: CX D->K and CX H->L, in parallel.
  T:   CX H->D, measure Z on K and L, in parallel.
Postselect the fourteen K/L records to zero. Discard H without measuring it.

The required pre-tail inner-code stabilizers are
  X_D X_H X_K X_L,  Z_D Z_K,  Z_H Z_L,
with inner logical X = X_D X_K and logical Z = Z_H Z_D.
Encode the outer Steane qubit across these seven inner qubits.

The resulting code has 28 physical qubits, one logical qubit and distance six.
Exact binary checks show 27 independent commuting stabilizers. The two final
layers map them to the six Steane output stabilizers and X_H,Z_K,Z_L on each
tile. Logical X and Z map to the required Steane logical operators on D.
Exact coset-weight enumeration gives minimum logical weights X=6,Z=6,Y=9.

With the pre-tail encoded input supplied perfectly, the complete noisy two-layer
transition gives
  L_tail = 2.192096391818694e-5
  A_tail = .9787606162143908.

This includes D2 noise after all21 CXs and p readout flips on all14 measurements.
There are no idle wires within either of these two layers. Postmeasurement
noise is irrelevant to the discarded measured qubits. The calculation is
checked by explicit 16^3 x 4 local Pauli/readout enumeration and an independent
conditional-channel formula, followed by all4^7 physical output Pauli patterns
through the exact Steane decoder. The leading rates are (X,Y,Z)=(10,6,6)p/15.

The ideal-boundary tail is 39.46% below full v37g and only .13% above the new
bound. This does NOT establish that a complete circuit obtains that gain.

5. THE REAL PREPARATION PROBLEM

The construction must prepare and verify the 28-qubit logical T state itself.
Encoding the old noisy seven-qubit prefix into these tiles and decoding it again
would preserve its existing outer-code errors. The fourteen final tile records
are not a replacement for missing outer-code syndrome verification.

For a full target of2.5e-5, the numerical gap above the ideal tail is3.079e-6;
for2.8e-5 it is6.079e-6. These are target differences, not additive certified
preparation-noise budgets. Full numerator N and acceptance A must be evaluated
together, including native preparation, waiting, routing and records.

The strengthened proof supplies a necessary preparation condition. For the
minimizing extracted all-X channel, let w be the nonzero-syndrome weight of its
conditional residual state. Then
  L >= L_star + .0031543241950135385 * w.
Consequently L<=2.5e-5 requires w<=.0009851515 (about.0985%), before paying any
logical misalignment inside the zero-syndrome sector. This w is not an arbitrary
raw-prefix syndrome histogram: its definition follows the exact factorization.

Geometry is also a concrete obstacle. The tile contacts fit the6x6 grid, but
that alone does not solve preparation routing. Exact enumeration finds242
disjoint seven-tile packings. Only12 leave eight connected free cells, and none
of those free regions touches all seven tiles; the maximum is five. Thus a
static28-data layout with a connected free-ancilla bus visiting every tile is
impossible in this grammar. This does not exclude moving or borrowed-data
preparation. The finite static-geometry check
in c4_tile_geometry.json distinguishes terminal packing from an accessible
ancillary verification workspace. Verification may have to occur before the
tiles are frozen, or use moving/borrowed data and changing gauge layouts.
All such costs remain to be constructed and measured.

6. CHANGE THE RESEARCH SUCCESS CRITERION

Current MERGE/EARLIER briefs accept improvements of3e-8, about.083% ofv37g.
That is a polishing criterion. A breakthrough lane needs a separate full-circuit
target, such as L<=2.8e-5 with exact A>=.5002 and legal official runtime.

The principal new experiment is a direct, verified28-qubit preparation or a
fault-tolerant growth process with sufficiently clean residual state, co-designed
with the two-layer contraction. Constrain the seven tiles only at the final
boundary, rather than freezing28 data positions throughout preparation.
Use the exact tail environment for N and A while choosing earlier checks and
source completion. Do not rank a full architecture by distance, gate count or
an isolated second-order coefficient.

As a lower-risk parallel branch, reducing prefix moves and verification costs
on a measured-partner architecture can still target roughly10-20%. Ordinary
output teleportation by itself is known to be fault-graph equivalent under
this noise model; it helps only if its geometry changes actual preparation,
movement and waiting costs. It does not supply a new lower-noise tail.

No complete circuit with a20-30% measured full-noise gain is claimed here.
The completed results are: current-v37g diagnostics; a new independently
audited stronger bound; an algebraically valid, native-grid terminal scaffold;
and its independently cross-checked exact finite-p tail loss. The outstanding
step is a sufficiently inexpensive, verified noisy preparation.
