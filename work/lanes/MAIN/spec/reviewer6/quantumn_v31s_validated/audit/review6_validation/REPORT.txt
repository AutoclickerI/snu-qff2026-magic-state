The review-5 A-decoder + SX candidate passes all four official validity
gates at 1,000,000 shots, seed 0. Its previous official class/correctness
gap is closed. Its runtime is poor: 163.306 seconds for the official check.

The fresh v31s A-only decoder candidate also passes all four gates at
1,000,000 shots and takes 2.669 seconds in the same local environment.
The unmodified v31s baseline takes 2.790 seconds. Thus the A-only decoder
has no observed judge-runtime regression.

Fresh v31s A+SX and two terminal operation reorderings pass all four gates
in bounded 10,000-shot checks, but retain a slow noisy-sampling path.
Class diagnosis, exact correctness, automatic zero-frame extraction, and
the noiseless cross-engine check are fast. Terminal reordering does not
restore the fast sampling path.

These are local runs of the actual official judge, not server submissions.
The final selected-circuit run uses the complete official score settings.
The shorter scores below are validation receipts, not precise estimates
for ranking close candidates.

The two reorder probes change only disjoint operations in the final native

native noisy streams. Both remain much slower than A-only. Their roughly
6-10 percent time variation does not establish removal of the slow path.

No further million-shot SX runs were launched. The three fast million-shot
checks were capped at 60 seconds each and completed in under 3 seconds.

Additional bounded controls replaced first-use MX preparations on pivots
26,17,28 at time 33 with RX and removed their carried measurement parities.
Both reset3 circuits pass official validity. A-only reset3 stays fast, but
the combined A+SX reset3 circuit remains slow. Separate all-orders pricing
by the tail lane finds both reset3 variants physically worse: A-only rises
from 4.94854579470615e-5 to 4.96160527277099e-5, and A+SX rises from
4.90979399123452e-5 to 4.93221815040179e-5. The original fast A-only circuit
therefore remains the chosen candidate.

The final Bell-flip control changes CX8->9 with MX8/M9 into CX9->8 with
M8/MX9 and swaps their detector record roles. It passes the official check
but still samples slowly. The bounded workaround search is closed. These
controls establish slow behavior of this combined A+SX family; they do not
prove that every circuit containing SX gates must be slow.


Native SX handling
------------------

The actual official Qiskit parser accepts sx and sxdg without expansion
into H/S/H. Native conversion contains seven SQRT_X and seven SQRT_X_DAG
instructions, preserving depth 48 for the fresh circuit. The official noise
generator attaches one DEPOLARIZE1(0.001) to each native SX instruction.
The forward gates occupy native times 43 and 44, and inverse gates occupy
time 47. See native_sx_noise_audit.json.

The fresh A+SX circuit contains 191 CX, 58 detectors, 35 M, 33 MX, 13 R,
25 RX, 8 T, and 7 T_DAG instructions. The official exact verifier and xtim's
noiseless cross-engine check both pass on the actual parsed circuit, so the
independent model's SX convention is not being relied upon for ideal
correctness validation.

Reproduction and recommended use
-------------------------------

Run from the workspace root, substituting the desired existing stem:

OPENBLAS_NUM_THREADS=1 OMP_NUM_THREADS=1 MKL_NUM_THREADS=1 \
audit/review6_validation/venv/bin/python \
audit/review6_validation/run_official.py 1000000 0 \
audit/review6_architecture/v31s_a_decode

For the final official-setting score, replace 1000000 with 100000000 and
keep seed 0. The selected QASM is also copied here as v31s_a_decode.qasm,
with adjacent v31s_a_decode.json metadata and the detailed
v31s_a_decode.validated.json receipt summary.

run_official.py calls the unmodified official tprep.check. Later runs add
transparent timing wrappers around the official functions without changing
their inputs, outputs, or exceptions. The first review-5 million-shot run
predates these wrappers and has total time only.

summary.json combines the local receipts. Each *.official_<shots>_<seed>.json
contains the exact QASM hash, versions, full Report fields, all validity
gate booleans, native counts, timings, and peak memory. Fixed QASM snapshots
and actual official native text are retained beside them.

Practical recommendation: prioritize the officially validated A-only
candidate. Keep SX as a physically slightly better, but significantly more
expensive-to-sample variant unless the slow sampling behavior is resolved.
The tail and parent lanes own the independent all-orders physical-loss
comparison; the official low-shot estimates here should not re-rank it.

All 13 completed official receipts pass their requested validity gates.
The final 100,000,000-shot run completed within its 330-second local bound,
using one process. Peak resident memory was 1,191,736 KiB. All scoped jobs
are finished and optional tests have stopped.
