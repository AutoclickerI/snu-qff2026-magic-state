1. STRONGEST NEW CANDIDATE: HELPER-ASSISTED DISCONNECTED B/C PREPARATION
   PLUS DELAYED MEASURED-BUS VERIFICATION

Exact ideal identity, with h initially |0>:
  CX(a,h); CX(h,b); MX(h)=m
implements CX(a,b) and a known Z_a^m byproduct. Both a and b survive.
For k controls and l targets, computing their parity on h, fanning it out, and
measuring h replaces 2k+l coherent CX operations by k+l CX plus one MX.
instrument_identities.py checks each Kraus matrix for (k,l)=(1,1),(2,1),(2,2),(3,1).

For a B-encoder bridge the Z byproduct remains a Z pattern inside B through
later CSS gates. B->C and B->data leave Z_B on B, so its final MX readouts can
absorb this known pattern by changing detector parities. A C-encoder Z pattern
may propagate to B during B->C; it likewise only changes later B MX records.
This can support disconnected B/C final-cell layouts using transient helpers.
Do not apply arbitrary physical Pauli output corrections: those are disallowed.

Pipelined remote B->C verification, for each i:
  R:   D_i->A_i, B_i->h_i
  R+1: B_i->D_i, h_i->C_i, MZ(A_i)
  R+2: MX(B_i), MX(h_i), MZ(C_i)
Use B's readout XOR h's readout in the B syndrome parities. C's encoder can
finish at R, whereas ordinary C->h teleport followed by B->h check needs it
finished at R-2. B's dangerous X information is copied before B->data and
verified afterwards. Data coupling times and final global depth are unchanged.

This is NOT a CX saving over ordinary one-CX C teleport plus one-CX check.
It saves CX versus coherent uncomputation and offers later C preparation and
helper reuse. It also does NOT beat an ideal adjacent one-CX direct check in
noise. The important trade is a small measured noise cost for geometry freedom.

Noise identity, proved with rational arithmetic in bus_noise_equivalence.py:
after folding h's MX into B's MX, the first B->h depolarizing channel is exactly
the direct B->C channel on (data X, B MX flip, C MZ flip). The added h->C channel,
h reset and h MX add only a classical correlated B/C readout-flip channel.
At p=.001 its probabilities (no flip, C only, B only, both) are
  .9972031322666667, .0012646010666666667,
  .0012646010666666667, .0002676656.
Its first-order rejection mass across seven pairs is .0196.

2. NEW RESEARCH CONTRACT: LOGICAL-BRANCH-EQUIVARIANT BC (AND A) ENDPOINT

Current BCSYN2 ft.py fixes B=|0_L> and a positive pure-CSS C endpoint. The
BCPOL U subset S search still fixes the logical coset. Instead permit
  (|0_L 0_L> + |1_L 1_L>)/sqrt(2)
on B,C; measure C in Z, check its three Hamming syndromes, permit either logical
parity b, and set FRAME_X ^= b. Every one of the 16 accepted C codewords has
probability 1/16 and leaves B=|b_L>. B->data gives logical X^b, canceled by frame.
instrument_identities.py verifies all 16 branches exactly, not just one branch.

For final X-error patterns e_B,e_C define
  ell(e_B) = parity(e_B) XOR [H e_B != 0].
After accepting H e_C=0, decoded X loss is governed by
  ell(e_B) XOR parity(e_C).
Thus matched logical-X errors on B and C can be benign: X_L^B X_L^C stabilizes
the encoded Bell endpoint. This changes the correlated-fault constraint that
makes the existing joint B/C synthesis expensive. It is not merely dropping
the C logical-parity detector. An independent logical-X fault on C passes its
three syndromes but makes the frame wrong; its T-state loss is exactly .5.
Require a new full-Pauli fault contract and exact all-branch correctness.

Analogously, a known logical-X eigenvalue of A=|+/-_L> can become FRAME_Z.
This is one family of instrument freedoms, not a separately counted candidate.

Cheap existing-network seed test was negative: on v29's 87-CX endpoint5 network,
the minimum input X subset producing logical X on both B,C after the network
has weight 5, with solutions (4,6,12,17,25) and (6,12,25,28,29). The older 82-CX
network also needs weight 5. No free one-pivot patch exists. Fresh synthesis is
required; no performance gain is claimed yet.

3. NEW TOY-SUPPORTED CANDIDATE: SELECTIVE ASYMMETRIC TEMPORARY STORAGE

During unavoidable data waiting, protect only a chosen 1–3 data positions with
native-|+> helper h, CX(h,data), wait, CX(h,data), MX(h) and reject minus.
This catches most Z/Y storage faults while leaving amplified X storage noise.
It must fit inside existing waiting; added round delay can erase the benefit.

Ideal-wrapper asymptotic mechanism: with per-Pauli storage probability a,
unprotected Steane T storage has 14 a^3 accepted logical loss. For protected
set P the coefficient is
  (1/2) sum_{Fano lines L} 2^{|P intersect L|}
    + (3/2) #{Fano lines disjoint from P}.
One protected position gives 11; two give 10; three forming a Fano line give 10
and remove every pure-Z logical line. Protecting all seven gives 28: it is worse.
This is why selective asymmetric protection, rather than blanket encoding,
is the candidate. Choose positions by actual A-danger/data-wait cross terms.

Finite-p toy includes all helper prep, two noisy CXs, every data/helper idle,
helper MX error and all 4^7 data patterns. Its FINAL output stabilizer checks
are IDEAL AND POSTSELECTED; it is NOT the full contest circuit.

must be priced in a full circuit. The isolated storage absolute gain is small;
the potentially larger mechanism is suppressing A-dangerous-Z times data-idle-Z
cross terms. Existing studies shortened waits or moved states; this changes the
physical channel during a wait. Failure gate: no full noisy improvement within
acceptance and unchanged round depth, or no legal helper lifetime/adjacency.

4. FRESH NEGATIVE SCREEN: JOINT DETECTOR KERNEL + LOGICAL FRAME ADMISSION

Old DET used fixed frames; BUDGET tested 139 variants with frame changes only
on12 standalone initialization records. I screened actual v29c's 993 supported
single-fault syndromes. 449 nonzero syndromes have a nonidentity I/X/Z/Y logical
frame making all accepted singles harmless. That first-order fact is misleading.
Exact distinct-channel pair enumeration for every 449 rank-one acceptance
kernel K={0,v} found ZERO with favorable screening objective
  added_pair_loss - 6.3451e-5 * added_single_probability_mass.
The best leading conditional risk of the newly admitted sector was .0036,
far above the baseline ~6.35e-5. Baseline exact second order 4.94155555556e-5.
This closes these 449 rank-one extensions at this screen, not all higher-rank
kernels or future circuits. Do not promote plain frame-based admission as a
new performance recommendation. New checks could disambiguate pair faults,
but that is a different architecture.

5. REDISCOVERY EXCLUDED

All 15 current deferred rotations commute, their reachable X rank is 5, and
their odd-mask vector can be reduced to one odd term modulo an affine
Reed-Muller relation. This is already TREP/TREP2 (FINDINGS rows 58/69): the 1T
form has a harmful single and 14T forms have harmful pairs. Not a new candidate.
