# Independent architecture and reproducibility audit

## Knill-style encoded Bell receiver

The potentially missed architecture is not simply replacing the last gates of the incumbent: prepare an encoded Bell pair as a jointly verified physical resource, destructively measure an incoming Steane block and one Bell half in complementary bases, use the six syndrome relations to postselect, and retain the other Bell half as the live output with its logical X and Z byproducts correctly represented. The receiver can be prepared concurrently and its interface can change the output locations. It therefore deserves a distinct complete-resource test.

Knill's original [postselected computation schemes](https://arxiv.org/abs/quant-ph/0402171) depend on error-detecting operations and sufficiently clean entangled resources; the paper does not supply a native 6×6 score under this judge. Goto's [step-by-step magic-state encoding](https://www.nature.com/articles/srep07501) uses a Bell ancilla within a concatenated-code construction and a 14-to-2 H6 distillation protocol, not a directly comparable seven-data, 36-cell live-output circuit. Goto's [Steane preparation work](https://www.nature.com/articles/srep19578) addresses compact verified encoded states, while [automated CSS state-preparation synthesis](https://journals.aps.org/prxquantum/abstract/10.1103/PRXQuantum.6.020330) targets spreading-error verification. These papers support resource-construction ideas; their reported overhead or error figures cannot replace an all-noise candidate evaluated on the actual judge.

The physically decisive questions are:

1. Does the noisy prepared Bell resource retain the needed correlations after its own flags and shared-cell scheduling, and does the verification distinguish resource faults from source syndrome history? A perfect Bell pair is an informative counterfactual, not a preparation recipe or monotonic bound.
2. Do physical records determine all six output stabilizer signs and the two logical byproducts? A whole-code Pauli frame handles logical byproducts, not arbitrary physical syndrome signs. Rejecting both random logical Bell outcomes would keep only one quarter of ideal shots under the standard ideal Bell measurement and cannot meet the 0.5 acceptance gate. Any frame or coherent correction must be validated by the actual judge contract.
3. Are the output idle clocks, record latency, Bell-resource wiring, measurement noise, flags and extra resets included? The receiver must remain live and raw-correct before decoding; destructive logical tomography or a noiseless late frame is not a substitute.
4. Can an admissible complete native implementation beat the incumbent's exact 3.566732196898089e-5 at A>=0.5002? The numerical board key is a sampled score rather than the competitor's exact expectation.

Existing results already delimit the opportunity. `SOTA_DETECT_20261028/REPORT.md` reports an ideal just-in-time one-bit Z-frame terminal of 2.5576079222320233e-5 under its explicitly artificial input/resource assumptions, but the corresponding actual independent noisy source/receiver control was 2.986875356753846e-4 with abstract connectivity. The literal native role reversal was raw-invalid owing to three output syndrome signs tied to source records; `JIT_RECEIVER_20261028/REPORT.md` records a repaired eligible frame-aware variant at 3.724715371413278e-5. These are distinct physical instruments and do not globally disprove a correlated encoded-Bell architecture. The completed `KNILL_HEADROOM_20261028` lane finds 2.9810308966e-5 with the real noisy source, ideal resource and one explicitly suppressed output idle; `KNILL_RESOURCE_20261028` prices a real verified-resource abstract construction at 6.0643544946e-5. Neither is a competitive complete native Bell circuit, and neither excludes a different jointly prepared native resource.

The recent direct-source controls close a different loophole: omitting the terminal altogether does not produce a competitive live output for the specific native, seven-cat abstract, or Gao sources tested. Raw correctness, single-fault exposure and complete all-noise prices, not circuit labels alone, determine that conclusion. See the direct lane reports and the root continuation report for current results.

## Direct C4 tile checks

The independent `c4_identity_audit.py` verifies the proposed tile's exact stabilizers and logical gates: XXXX, Z_D Z_K, Z_H Z_L; logical X_D X_K and Z_H Z_D; and logical T via CX(H,D), T(D), CX(H,D). A cat-controlled logical X can use CX(D,K), CX(cat,D), CX(D,K), which equals the two abstract controls on D and K while using only the cat–D and D–K edges. Its extra physical fault location must be priced. A stationary cat cannot directly touch both adjacent D and K on a square grid, which has no triangles.

The `c4_geometry_audit.py` exhaustive static test checks all 242 packings of seven 2×2 tiles in the 6×6 grid. Twenty-eight admit seven distinct exterior cat cells individually adjacent to tile corners; none admits those seven matched cat cells as a connected set. The scope is fixed tiles and cats, with any tile corner as D and no additional entangling bus or movement. This does not rule out time-dependent layouts or using the eighth spare cell as a bus. The 28 live tile cells plus seven cat cells leave exactly one spare cell during each cat round; seven extra syndrome meters cannot be simultaneously resident. These geometry and ideal identities are not a full noisy preparation or an architecture bound.
