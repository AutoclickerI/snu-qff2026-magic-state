# Architectural assessment

The verified native reference is L=3.566732196898089e-5. A 19% expected-loss reduction requires L≤2.8890530794874525e-5, a reduction of 6.77679e-6. Relative to the documented universal terminal-noise floor near 2.18925e-5, that would remove about 49% of the reference's remaining gap. The leaderboard key 2.86759593420732e-5 observed 2026-10-27 16:23:30 UTC is a sampled draw, so comparing it directly with an expected loss does not establish a rank.

**Assessment:** no tested new family demonstrates that reduction on the grid. A resource-first CP-SAT search is useful for constructing candidates, but its objective cannot see a structural gain excluded by the chosen state boundary, and a minimum gate count or c1=0 does not predict conditional full-noise loss. The ULO direct-check experiment reduced the reconstructed abstract seven-cat loss only from 2.97208e-5 to 2.96499e-5 after scheduling; the specific ULO terminal replacement had harmful singles and was far worse. These tests do not rule out all nondestructive instruments, but they give no evidence that inserting another standalone check is a 19% native lever.

The measured-gauge source families also need the entire unmeasured output and its terminal noise included; the Gao 15-to-7 construction in the source lane was fully reconstructed but its best complete abstract judge score was 4.5215039968e-5, so its paper's destructive diagnostic is not a transferable bound. Likewise, literal one-bit output relocation of the existing native B block fails raw code-space correctness, and the fresh-source control costs 3.72376e-5 at acceptance 0.49767. A full encoded-Bell terminal with perfect resources costs 4.62958e-5 in the tested two-idle layout. None of these results rules out a redesigned, jointly prepared teleportation instrument.

The one specific untested high-leverage experiment I would retain is **joint preparation of a native dual one-bit teleport receiver and syndrome meter**, co-synthesized with their source records rather than adapting the incumbent B factory. Prepare A as encoded plus and B as encoded zero; perform D→A with Z reads of A, then D→B with X reads of old D, retaining B as output and using the old-D logical parity as the legal Z frame. Design the real B preparation so its final physical operation overlaps the first D→A check, and co-design the A/B state and their deterministic measurement contract so all raw B code stabilizers are +1. The conceptual use of teleportation with error detection is established, for example by Knill's [2005 stabilizer-code teleportation work](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevA.71.042322); this proposed joint native implementation has not been demonstrated here.

This deserves a single bounded architecture test because the *explicitly counterfactual* ideal JIT terminal has L=2.5576079222320233e-5, close to the ideal square terminal, while the same terminal with an additional receiver idle costs 3.41962e-5. The difference is a concrete schedule-sensitive mechanism, not an achieved gain: no perfect final preparation exists, and the fully noisy unverified receiver test costs 2.98688e-4. A useful next experiment must generate actual 6×6 nearest-neighbor preparations and checks, include both their accepted residue and all gate/read/wait noise, verify raw code-space output and the affine frame with the pinned judge, and price complete L and A≥0.5002. A matched source plus ideal JIT screen may prioritize candidates, but only a complete native price can establish the requested reduction. If a realistic JIT receiver cannot be produced within the 36-cell/source-release constraints, stop this family rather than treating the ideal number as a result.
