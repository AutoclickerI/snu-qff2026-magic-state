## 1. What the floors actually assume

The judge inserts participant noise before appending its ideal syndrome round (`tprep/check.py:189–203`, under `upstream/snu-qff2026-problem/`). Acceptance uses participant records; the ideal syndrome never rejects (`check.py:273–302`). Its correction gives logical X/Y loss 1/2 and logical Z loss 1 on a Pauli-corrupted target. Noise follows native ASAP timing, including data waits to the final operation (`noise.py:54–88,106–122`). Those are firm constraints; a prescribed Steane round or fault-scan threshold is not (`problem.md:263–307,559–563`).

| Claimed floor / assumption | Test and status |
|---|---|
| Any circuit ≥ `1.116e-5` | **Not proved by `work/bounds.py`.** It enumerates a product of seven chosen single-qubit channels (`:44–66`), and compares idle with one checked-dummy-CX recipe (`:69–93`). It neither enumerates arbitrary last instruments nor minimizes over their conditional input states. `docs/FINDINGS.md:116` already acknowledges the universal-proof gap. |
| Perfect code state before an independent terminal Pauli layer | Holds for the isolated-tail diagnostic. A full accepted noisy circuit can have pre-existing syndromes, correlations and changed logical Bloch vectors. Removing earlier noise is a counterfactual, not automatically a monotone lower-bound operation for conditional fidelity. A proof must optimize over these input states, or bound their effect. |
| Every output must idle once | Holds for **this circuit**: data’s final CXs are at step 46 and readouts finish at 47 (`v23h_c2c9.qasm:390–415`, under `work/lanes/MAIN/`). Not universal: a terminal identity CX with a suitable partner replaces idle with a different marginal channel. Preparing/checking that partner also costs noise. |
| Idle versus checked dummy exhausts the best ending | Unproved. **M:** cutoff-free enumeration reproduces idle **1.1625252533e-5** and the assumed dummy channel **1.1158788605e-5** (`floors.json`). These verify arithmetic, not attainability or optimality. `bounds.py:61–62` also drops tiny probabilities; its “exact” discrepancy is only about **2.2e-11**, immaterial here. |
| Direct readout ≥ `2.567e-5` | Correct **second-order data-side accounting for the stated class**: each data wire has its last complementary check CXs in consecutive steps, separate single-basis ancilla readout, then an idle; invisible ancilla faults have mass `2p/15` (`bounds.py:8–15,74–82`). M: optimizing its X/Z order masks gives **2.5666666667e-5**, attained by all X-last. |
| “Exact” direct floor `2.553e-5` | M: **2.5529889994e-5** for the supplied independent, linearly added tail masses. Those masses omit channel-composition/conditioning corrections; it is not an all-orders evaluation of a physical complete round. For our actual round, M is **2.8712542222e-5 through third order**. |
| Readout copying, flags or teleportation must respect that same direct bound | Only if their causal ending reduces to the same two-check/product-tail model. Joint verification/readout instruments, different output-transfer boundaries, and correlated fault filtering require a new derivation. No tested alternative here beats the direct round’s physical loss; “escapes the proof’s assumptions” does not mean “has a better circuit.” `work/lanes/ARCH/REPORT.md:42–44` explicitly limits its negative claims. |

**A broader terminal-layer relaxation (I derivation, M enumeration):** at the final native tick, a valid output qubit cannot end in measurement/reset, since the noiseless accepted target is entangled across every output cut (`problem.md:274–307`). Each output therefore has a gate/idle depolarizer, or belongs to a final CX. An output–discarded-ancilla CX contributes marginal `DEPOLARIZE1(4p/5)`; an output–output CX contributes `DEPOLARIZE2(p)`. Final record measurements are disjoint from those operations, so conditioning only chooses an arbitrary pre-noise output density matrix. Relax every single-qubit `D1(p)` to `D1(4p/5)`; the extra depolarization can be absorbed into that arbitrary input state.

M: enumerate all **232** partitions of the output into singletons/pairs and the **64** syndrome sectors of the recovery adjoint. In each sector maximize fidelity using `(1+hypot(C_X,C_Y)/sqrt(2))/2`. Minimum terminal losses with **0/1/2/3** output–output CXs are **7.4454535e-6 / 3.3758468e-4 / 6.6840459e-4 / 9.9990591e-4** (`audit.py:105–129`, `floors.json`). An independent dense **128×128** recovery-adjoint eigenvalue calculation agrees within M **2.7e-15** for the all-singleton and one-pair cases (`terminal_matrix.json`). This supports a weaker universal terminal-noise bound of approximately **7.44545e-6**, with the derivation just stated; floating-point enumeration is not an interval-arithmetic certificate. It supplies **no construction** near that bound and does **not** disprove a stronger `1.116e-5` theorem using earlier-time constraints. It shows why the existing two-recipe calculation does not establish that theorem.

## 4. Missing or underused methods

Every gain/effort range below is **I**, an engineering forecast of possible absolute reduction from v23 after successful implementation; **zero gain is possible for every row**. Ranges overlap and are not additive. They are based on the ledger/receipts, not transferred literature headline improvements. First experiments are scoped to less than one day on the allowed resources.

| Method and current gap | Why it can escape the present family | Gain range and basis | First experiment; effort |
|---|---|---|---|

Avoid exponential contraction-width blowups. **2–8 h** integration. |
| **Branch-and-bound with valid bounds** | Certifies small subproblems and excludes whole topology/endpoint families; current solver optimality is usually for gate/depth proxies, not conditional fidelity. | **0–0.5e-5** first-day physical gain; primary return is useful certificates and search pruning. No empirical basis for a large immediate win. | Branch on a small joint A/check window; canonical stabilizer-state BFS gives remaining-CX/depth lower bounds, then exact leaf loss. Important optimization detail (I): for target loss `τ`, use `N−τ A` with `A≥0.5` to formulate a conditional-loss decision problem. Noise removal, early-round witnesses, and truncated Taylor values are not automatically admissible branch-and-bound certificates. Lagrangian prices are heuristic unless a global lower bound is actually obtained for each relaxed problem.

## 5. Ranked action and single recommendation

By immediate gain/effort: **(1)** integrate KNAP’s already verified runtime/acceptance repair and resolve close variants with all-orders/adequate re-draw means; **(2)** use the existing BCPOL response cache for joint endpoint/prep/check candidates; **(3)** open prefix relocation and layout jointly with those endpoints; **(4)** fault-weighted schedule/acceptance search; **(5)** ZX/alternative prefix synthesis; **(6)** full score branch-and-bound or rare-event MC. This ordering is I, and distinguishes quick reliable gains from the larger structural bet.

**Single direction toward true `1-F < 5e-5`: joint dynamic placement and instrument synthesis across the cultivation-to-Steane boundary.** Keep the useful logical cultivation construction initially, but permit its data wires to move before their last prefix interactions, retarget those interactions, and choose A/B/C/C6 roles and preparations jointly. Preserve the accepted-state/detector contract and price complete circuits with cubic/Walsh evaluation. This addresses both M **1.80e-5** grid-free replacement contrast and M **2.08e-5** data-exposure removal price; they overlap and cannot be added. A standalone better B+C prep cannot be assumed to bridge the target gap: even making its current noise vanish leaves M **5.2475e-5 through third order**.

Under-one-day success criterion (proposal, not a new standing rule): obtain a physically emitted, noiselessly correct candidate whose full third-order loss is below **4.8e-5**, then check all-orders/MC and actual acceptance/runtime; or deliver a measured relaxation ladder and explicitly bounded model certificate identifying the remaining obstacle. The **4.8e-5** screen is an I working margin against omitted orders, not a guarantee. A smaller R, fewer CX, SAT witness, or isolated block win alone is insufficient. No evidence currently warrants promising a true score near `1e-5`.
