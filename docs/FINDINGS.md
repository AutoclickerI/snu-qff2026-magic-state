## Mechanisms (design rules)

Only single faults giving a zero-syndrome Z_L pattern on A are dangerous: a Z_L×(weight ≤ 1) class on A plus one data Z on the third qubit of that logical line = undetected Z_L (danger `synth.dangerous_z`) | mechanism | model | `work/lanes/DR/{steane,synth}.py` |
